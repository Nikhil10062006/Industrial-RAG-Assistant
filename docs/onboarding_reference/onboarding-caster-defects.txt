Continuous Caster - Surface Defect Quick ID
As a strand operator, you inspect the hot slabs as they exit the torch cutter. Knowing the difference between defects saves entire campaigns.

Longitudinal Cracks: Running lengthwise down the slab. Usually caused by uneven mold cooling or bad mold powder. Check the mold water delta-T.

Transverse Cracks: Running across the width. Usually caused by bending the slab too cold. Tell the pulpit to reduce secondary spray cooling water in zone 3.

Oscillation Marks: Deep ripples on the surface. Means the mold oscillator frequency is out of sync with casting speed. Check the HPU pressure.

Bleeders: Small bumps of frozen metal on the surface. Usually means the steel is too hot (high superheat). Warn the melt shop to cool the next ladle.
Mark defects boldly with chalk so the scarfing yard can find them.
