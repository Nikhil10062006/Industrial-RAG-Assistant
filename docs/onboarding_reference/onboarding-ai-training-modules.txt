Mandatory Training: Utilizing AI Diagnostic Tools
As a new shift technician, the sheer scale of the plant's automation networks can be overwhelming. To accelerate your troubleshooting capabilities without risking live equipment, you are required to use the internal simulation platforms.
There is a file you can reference named "Agentic AI based learning and development". You must review this file in your first week. It outlines how our AI correlates real-time HMI alarms with historical maintenance logs to predict failures.
For example, if the Hot Strip Mill descale pump trips on high vibration, the AI tool will flag whether this vibration signature historically matches a cavitating impeller or a failing thrust bearing. Use these tools to formulate a hypothesis before you request a major mechanical intervention. Do not blindly follow the AI if the physical evidence (e.g., a massive pool of water, or the smell of burning wiring) contradicts it, but always use it as your first point of reference for complex, multi-variable faults.
