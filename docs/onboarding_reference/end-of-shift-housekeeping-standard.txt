Housekeeping is part of equipment reliability and safety. Before handover, remove loose scale, welding rods, rags, oil containers, scrap, temporary hoses, and tools from walkways, platforms, cabinets, drains, and crane routes. Do not push waste into a drain or under a machine.

Check that guards, covers, access gates, handrails, lights, spill kits, extinguishers, and emergency showers are accessible. Coil and plate edges must be stored in the designated orientation. Loose refractory, hot slag, and wet scrap need the correct cooling or disposal route.

For maintenance completion, account for every tool and temporary support. Confirm blind flanges, jumpers, test leads, and tags are either removed or documented. A forgotten wrench inside a guard or a rag on a hot motor can become a failure at startup.

Photograph or record abnormal housekeeping rather than silently correcting a recurring problem. If a drain repeatedly blocks with scale or a platform repeatedly collects oil, assign an owner and corrective action. A clean floor helps, but a corrected source helps more.
