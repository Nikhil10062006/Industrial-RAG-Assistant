Basic Instrument Air Network Map
Instrument air is clean, dried air used purely for pneumatics and control valves. Plant air is dirty, wet air used for tools and blowing. Never mix them.
The Ring Main: The instrument air network runs in a complete circle around the plant. This means if a pipe breaks in one area, you can isolate it, and air will flow backwards around the ring to feed the units.
Priority Consumers: If compressor capacity drops, we shed load.

Priority 1 (Never cut): Blast Furnace, BOF lances, Caster cooling.

Priority 2 (Cut if desperate): Rolling mill pneumatic guides, Sinter plant dump valves.

Priority 3 (Cut first): Maintenance shop air (even if they complain).
The dew point must remain below -40°C. If the air dryers fail, water gets into the valves, and everything will rust from the inside out within a week.
