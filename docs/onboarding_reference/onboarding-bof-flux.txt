BOF Flux Addition Cheat Sheet
Fluxes are added to the molten iron in the BOF to create slag, which absorbs impurities like sulfur and phosphorus.
The Main Players:

Calcined Lime (CaO): The primary flux. Highly reactive. Usually add between 4 to 6 tons per heat.

Dolomitic Lime (MgO): Added to protect the furnace refractory lining from being eaten by the slag. Usually 1 to 2 tons per heat.
Rules of Thumb:
If the hot metal silicon is high (>0.6%), you will need more lime to neutralize the acidic silica formed during the blow.
Watch the hopper weights. If the lime hopper takes longer than 30 seconds to discharge 1 ton, the bin is likely bridged. Tap the side of the bin with a sledgehammer or activate the bin vibrators. Never blow oxygen without a proper flux charge, or you will destroy the vessel lining in one heat.
