Blast Furnace Stave Cooling - Junior Guide
The blast furnace is lined with copper and cast iron cooling panels called staves. If they lose water, they melt in minutes, and the furnace shell burns through.
Normal Parameters: The cooling water enters at roughly 35°C and exits at 40°C. A normal delta-T is 3 to 5 degrees Celsius.
What to look for:

Delta-T > 10°C: The stave is absorbing too much heat. The burden inside might be channeling gas up the walls. Report it to the blower immediately.

Delta-Flow Drop: If flow drops but supply pressure is normal, the stave pipe might be scaling up inside, or boiling (vapor lock) has occurred.
Valve Locations: The supply headers are on the ring balcony at elevation +15m. Do not throttle any valve without direct orders from the shift foreman.
