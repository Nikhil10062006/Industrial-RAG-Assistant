Quick Reference: Forging Shop Terminology
When reading the Standard Operating Procedures for Iron and Steel Forging, familiarize yourself with these terms.

Billet: The raw block of steel.

Upsetting: Hitting the billet on its end to make it shorter and wider.

Preforming: Shaping the metal roughly before it goes into the final die.

Scale: The hard, black iron oxide that forms on red-hot steel. It must be blasted off using the Standard Operating Procedure for Shot Blasting and Surface Cleaning (SOP-821-008).
