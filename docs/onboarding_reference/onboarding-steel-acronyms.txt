Quick Reference: Essential Plant Acronyms
Welcome to the plant. You will hear these terms constantly. Learn them.

BOF (Basic Oxygen Furnace): Where we turn iron into steel by blowing oxygen.

BF (Blast Furnace): The massive tower that turns iron ore into liquid iron.

HSM (Hot Strip Mill): The rolling mill that flattens thick steel slabs into thin coils.

TCM (Tandem Cold Mill): Rolls pickled steel at room temperature to make it ultra-thin and strong.

VFD (Variable Frequency Drive): The electronic box that controls the speed of AC motors.

LOTO (Lockout/Tagout): The safety procedure of locking off power before touching machinery.

HMI (Human Machine Interface): The computer screens the operators use in the pulpits.

PLC (Programmable Logic Controller): The industrial computer making the automated decisions.
