Mandatory Crane Hand Signals
The Melt Shop is too loud for radios sometimes. You must know the physical hand signals to communicate with the crane operator 30 meters above you.

Hoist Up: Right arm extended vertically, pointing finger up, moving hand in small horizontal circles.

Lower: Right arm extended downward, pointing finger down, moving hand in small horizontal circles.

Bridge Travel: Arm extended forward, hand open and slightly raised, making a pushing motion in the direction of travel.

Trolley Travel: Palm up, fingers closed, thumb pointing in the direction of motion, jerking hand horizontally.

Stop: Arm extended, palm down, moving arm back and forth horizontally.

Emergency Stop: Both arms extended, palms down, moving arms rapidly back and forth horizontally. The crane operator will drop whatever they are doing if they see this.
