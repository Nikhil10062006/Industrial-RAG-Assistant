Utility Dispatch - Shift Handover Protocol
The utility desk controls the lifeblood of the plant: Steam, Power, and Gas. Your shift handover must be precise.
Record these metrics in the logbook at 15 minutes to shift end:

Gas Holder Levels: LD Gas holder must be > 40%. Coke Oven Gas holder > 60%. If levels are dropping, you must warn the reheat furnaces to curtail usage.

Steam Header Pressure: Main header must be strictly at 40 bar. If it's 38 bar and dropping, ask the power plant to fire up the auxiliary boiler.

Grid Import: Note the MW import from the state grid. If we are near our peak demand penalty threshold, prepare to shed non-essential loads like the raw material crushers.
Talk to the outgoing dispatcher. Ask them specifically: "Are there any manual bypasses open right now?"
