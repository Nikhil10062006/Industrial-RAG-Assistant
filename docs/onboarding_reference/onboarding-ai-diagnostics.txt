Leveraging the Plant Diagnostic Base
As a junior technician, you are not expected to know every failure mode of a 40-year-old steel plant. However, you are expected to know how to find the answer quickly before escalating.
We have digitized thousands of shift logs, schematics, and root-cause analysis reports. When you encounter a complex fault—like a cascading hydraulic pressure drop or an obscure VFD fault code—do not guess.
You must log into the main terminal and query the system. Make sure you load up the Agentic AI based learning and development file. This module cross-references historical breakdowns with live telemetry. Use it to simulate the fault on your tablet before you start turning wrenches on the actual machinery. If the AI suggests a fix you don't fully understand, that is the exact moment you radio a veteran supervisor for guidance.
