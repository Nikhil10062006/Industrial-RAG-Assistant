Open permits must be handed over face to face or through the approved control process. State the permit number, equipment, work scope, isolation status, gas-test result, people present, tools or blinds installed, and the condition required before work resumes.

Do not assume the incoming shift accepts a permit because it appears on a screen. The incoming supervisor must understand the boundary and verify the field where required. If conditions changed, the permit must be suspended or revalidated.

For confined spaces, record entrant and attendant status, last gas test, ventilation, rescue equipment, and next test time. For hot work, record fire-watch status and post-work watch period. For lifting, record load position, crane status, and exclusion zone.

Close or transfer permits according to the site rule. Never leave a permit open because “the same job will continue tomorrow” without confirming the equipment remains isolated and the work party is known. Permit status is a safety control, not an administrative count.
