Plant Water Pumping Station - Basics for Freshers
The steel plant uses millions of liters of water an hour. Your job at the pump station is keeping the pressure headers stable.
Pump Start Sequence (Centrifugal Pumps):

Check the lube oil level on the bearing sight glass.

Ensure the suction valve is 100% OPEN. (Never start a pump with a closed suction valve; it will cavitate and destroy the impeller in minutes).

Ensure the discharge valve is 100% CLOSED. (Starting against a closed discharge valve minimizes the electrical starting current spike on the motor).

Start the motor. Watch the amp meter peak and drop.

Slowly crack open the discharge valve over 30 seconds until you hit the main header pressure setpoint (usually 4.5 bar).
Vibration: Put your hand on the motor casing (safely). You will learn what a "happy" pump feels like. If it feels like a bucket of gravel is inside it, the bearings are failing, or the pump is cavitating. Shut it down and swap to the standby.
