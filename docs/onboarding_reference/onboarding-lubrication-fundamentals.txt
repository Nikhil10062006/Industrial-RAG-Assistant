The Basics of Industrial Lubrication
Steel plant equipment works under extreme heavy load, very high speed, and high temperatures in dusty environments. You must use the correct grease.

Lithium EP2 Grease: The standard workhorse. Good for regular bearings.

High-Temperature Polyurea Grease: Used near the continuous casters where standard grease turns to liquid.

Molybdenum Disulfide (Moly) Grease: Used in slow-moving, heavily loaded pins.
Never mix grease types; mixing lithium-complex grease with polyurea grease chemically reacts into a solid mass that seizes the bearing.
