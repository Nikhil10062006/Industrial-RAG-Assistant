Hydraulic Schematics - The Basics
Don't let the spaghetti of lines on a hydraulic drawing intimidate you.

Solid Lines: Main pressure or return fluid lines.

Dashed Lines: Pilot pressure lines (small lines used to shift bigger valves).

Dotted Lines: Drain lines (leakage fluid returning to the tank).

The Box with an Arrow: A pump. The arrow points in the direction of flow.

The Box with a Z-line: A spring. Usually used to push a valve back to its default safe position when power is lost.
The Golden Rule: Fluid always takes the path of least resistance. If a cylinder won't move, look for the easiest place the fluid could be escaping back to the tank—usually a stuck relief valve or a blown internal seal. Trace the solid lines backward from the cylinder to find the culprit.
