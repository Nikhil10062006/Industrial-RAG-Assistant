Arc Flash boundary enforcement and PPE requirements for switching 33kV main distribution breakers in the Plant Primary Substation.
The arc flash boundary for 33kV primary switchgear is 8 meters. When a breaker is being racked in or out, or physically switched via local pendant, no personnel other than the designated switching operator are permitted within this boundary.
The switching operator must wear an Arc Flash Suit rated for a minimum of 40 cal/cm2 (Category 4). This includes the full hood, face shield, jacket, bib overalls, and voltage-rated rubber gloves with leather protectors. Never stand directly in front of the breaker cubicle doors during operation; stand to the side at the maximum extension of the switching pendant cable. Before racking a breaker, physically inspect the cubicle vents—if you hear a buzzing/hissing sound (corona discharge) or smell ozone, abort the operation immediately and notify the High Voltage maintenance team.
