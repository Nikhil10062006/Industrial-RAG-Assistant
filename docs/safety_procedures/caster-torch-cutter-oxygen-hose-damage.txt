Before using a torch cutter, inspect oxygen and fuel hoses for cuts, burns, flattened sections, loose fittings, and correct routing. Confirm flashback arrestors, regulators, check valves, torch body, and emergency shutoff are in place. Hoses must remain away from hot slabs, moving tables, sharp edges, and crane traffic. If a hose bubbles, smells of gas, or shows damage, close the supply from the safe point and remove it from service. Do not wrap tape around a pressurized hose or continue until the next shift. A hose can fail suddenly after heat has weakened the cover. Keep ignition sources controlled during inspection and use the approved leak-test solution. Record hose number, defect, gas service, isolation, replacement, and test result. After replacement, purge and light according to the burner procedure; never light from an unapproved source. The cutter area must remain clear of people below the slab drop and of wet material that can create steam.
