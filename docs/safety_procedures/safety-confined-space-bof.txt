Protocol for Confined Space Entry into a Basic Oxygen Furnace (BOF) vessel for refractory brick lining. This is a hazardous environment due to extreme residual heat, vertical drop risks, and falling slag.
The vessel must be locked in the vertical position using both electrical brakes and physical heavy steel locking pins inserted into the trunnion ring. The main oxygen and inert gas headers must be physically double-blocked and bled.
Before the reline platform (the "tower") can be lowered into the vessel, a specialized team must use a long pneumatic deskulling hammer from the outside to knock off any loose slag crust overhanging the vessel mouth. If a 100kg chunk of slag falls from the lip while men are working 10 meters below inside the vessel, it is fatal. Entrants must wear hard hats with chin straps to prevent them from falling off when looking up. Ventilation fans must run continuously to supply fresh air down the shaft.
