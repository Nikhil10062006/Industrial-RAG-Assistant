Before connecting a portable generator, confirm the approved connection point, transfer arrangement, earthing, cable rating, fuel, exhaust route, and isolation from the normal supply. Never connect a generator to a plant circuit through an improvised socket or without preventing backfeed. Test absence of voltage and verify the changeover device position. Keep the generator outdoors or in the approved ventilated location because exhaust gas can accumulate. Protect cables from hot surfaces, traffic, water, and sharp edges. Record generator identification, load, connection, earthing, transfer position, and responsible person. A generator that starts and powers one tool can still energize a circuit believed to be isolated. Removal requires the same discipline as installation. Do not refuel while hot or running, and control fuel spills immediately.

Before starting or restarting, confirm the responsible area owner, the boundary, the communication method, and the emergency route. Record the condition before the intervention and the result after it. Do not rely on a screen indication alone when a field check is possible. If weather, heat, gas, pressure, movement, or visibility changes, stop and reassess rather than continuing on the original assumption. The next shift should receive the equipment tag, time, readings, people informed, temporary controls, and the condition that requires a further stop.
