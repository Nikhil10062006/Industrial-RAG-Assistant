Mandatory cleanliness protocol for maintaining 40-bar Oxygen pipelines at the BOF station. Pure, pressurized oxygen is highly reactive. It lowers the auto-ignition temperature of organics to room temperature.
A single fingerprint of grease, a drop of lubricating oil, or a stray thread of cotton left inside a pipe will auto-ignite instantly when hit by high-velocity oxygen. This creates a localized metal fire that will burn straight through the carbon steel pipe wall.
All tools used on oxygen lines must be strictly degreased using an approved solvent (e.g., Trichloroethylene) and dried completely before use. Keep these tools in a separate, sealed toolbox; never mix them with general mechanical tools. When replacing a gasket, use only the designated blue PTFE oxygen-compatible gaskets. Technicians must wash their hands thoroughly and wear brand-new, clean leather gloves to handle internal valve components.
