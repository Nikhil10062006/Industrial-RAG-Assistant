Protocol for Confined Space Entry into a Blast Furnace Hot Blast Stove. Stoves are extreme high-risk confined spaces due to residual toxic gases (CO), heat, and oxygen deficiency.
Entry is absolutely prohibited until the stove has been isolated using the Double Block and Bleed method on all gas and cold blast mains. A physical slip blind must be installed on the gas burner inlet. The stove must be force-ventilated using explosion-proof blowers for a minimum of 24 hours prior to entry.
Before signing the entry permit, the safety officer must probe the atmosphere from top, middle, and bottom elevations. Acceptable limits are strictly: Oxygen between 19.5% and 23.5%, CO < 30 ppm, and LEL < 10%. Continuous atmospheric monitoring is required while personnel are inside. A trained hole-watch attendant must be stationed at the manhole at all times with a retrieval winch attached to the entrant's harness. Maximum stay time inside the stove is 30 minutes due to heat stress limits.
