Working at Height (WAH) on the Blast Furnace Bustle Pipe ring main. The bustle pipe distributes 1100°C hot blast air around the furnace. The walkway is elevated 15 meters above the cast house floor and is heavily congested with gas piping, water lines, and instrumentation.
Standard double-lanyard 100% tie-off rules apply, but anchor point selection is critical. Never, under any circumstances, use a gas pipe or a pressurized cooling water line as an anchor point. If you fall, the dynamic shock load of your body weight (up to 22kN) will snap a small-diameter gas pipe, causing a lethal CO gas leak directly into your face while you are suspended.
You may only anchor to structural steel columns, designated yellow-painted anchor lugs, or the heavy structural handrail stanchions (not the mid-rails). Due to the extreme radiant heat from the bustle pipe, synthetic fall-arrest lanyards can melt. Only Kevlar-webbed or wire-rope lanyards are permitted in the bustle pipe zone.
