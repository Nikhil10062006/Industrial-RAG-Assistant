High-pressure hydraulic injection hazard protocol. Many plant systems (Cold Mill AGC, Caster oscillators, Furnace hydraulics) run at pressures exceeding 200 bar.
A pinhole leak in a hydraulic hose running at 200 bar creates a micro-jet of oil that is completely invisible to the naked eye but acts like a scalpel. If you pass your hand over a pinhole leak to "feel" for oil, the jet will instantly cut through your leather work gloves and inject toxic hydraulic fluid directly into your bloodstream and muscle tissue.
Rule: Never use your hands to search for hydraulic leaks on live equipment.
If you suspect a leak on a pressurized block or hose, use a piece of stiff cardboard or a wooden broom handle. Sweep the cardboard around the suspected area at a safe distance. The invisible oil jet will slice or heavily stain the cardboard. If fluid injection occurs, it is an extreme medical emergency requiring immediate surgical excision; report to the plant hospital instantly and provide them with the MSDS for the hydraulic fluid.
