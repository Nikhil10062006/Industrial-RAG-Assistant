Zero Energy State Lockout-Tagout (LOTO) procedure for gravity-tensioned incline conveyors (e.g., Sinter Plant feed belts).
Locking out the electrical motor at the MCC is only the first step. Incline conveyors possess massive stored potential energy. Even with the motor dead, a loaded belt can run backward under the weight of the material if the mechanical anti-rollback backstop fails.
Before stepping onto the belt or reaching into the tail pulley, you must neutralize the mechanical energy.

The gravity take-up (counterweight) must be physically pinned or supported. If the belt snaps while you are working on it, a 5-ton counterweight will free-fall, whipping the belt violently. Insert the heavy steel support beams under the counterweight box.

Apply chain-blocks to physically chain the main drive pulley to the structural steel framework to prevent freewheeling.
Only when electrical power is locked, the counterweight is blocked, and the pulley is chained can the permit be issued to the vulcanizing team to repair a torn belt.
