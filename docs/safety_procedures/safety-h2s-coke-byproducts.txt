Hydrogen Sulfide (H2S) acute hazard protocol in the Coke By-Products (CBP) sulfur recovery plant. H2S is a lethal, heavier-than-air gas.
While it smells like rotten eggs at safe, low levels (1-5 ppm), it aggressively attacks and paralyzes your olfactory nerves at hazardous levels (>50 ppm). If you smell a strong odor of rotten eggs that suddenly disappears, you are not safe—you have lost your sense of smell, and the concentration is rising to lethal levels.
If your personal H2S monitor alarms at 10 ppm (Low), immediately put on your half-mask respirator and move cross-wind. If the alarm hits 20 ppm (High), it is a mandatory evacuation. Do not attempt to close valves or investigate the leak without a self-contained breathing apparatus (SCBA). If a colleague collapses in an H2S area, do not hold your breath and run in to drag them out; you will inevitably take a breath and become the second fatality. Wait for the SCBA rescue team.
