Zone-specific Personal Protective Equipment (PPE) requirements for the Steel Melting Shop (Melt Shop). The Melt Shop is divided into standard and high-risk zones.
In general walkways and the scrap yard, standard FR (Flame Resistant) cotton clothing, hard hat, safety glasses, and steel-toed metatarsal boots are required.
Within 5 meters of the BOF vessel, any live ladle, or the continuous caster mold (The "Red Zone"), secondary PPE is mandatory. This includes an aluminized radiant heat coat, aluminized leggings (spats), and heat-resistant Kevlar gloves. When observing the BOF blow or taking a dip-temperature sample, a gold-tinted face shield must be pulled down over the hard hat to protect against UV radiation and micro-splashes. Synthetic clothing (polyester, nylon) is strictly banned anywhere in the Melt Shop; it will melt into the skin upon exposure to extreme radiant heat. Check the integrity of aluminized gear daily; cracked reflective surfaces offer zero protection.
