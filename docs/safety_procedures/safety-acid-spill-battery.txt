Response procedure for a sulfuric acid spill in the main Substation UPS Battery Room. The battery banks contain hundreds of liters of high-concentration acid.
If a cracked jar or spilled acid is discovered, do not step in the liquid. Evacuate the room and leave the door open to prevent hydrogen gas buildup. Put on full chemical-resistant PPE: acid-proof apron, heavy rubber gloves, chemical goggles, and a face shield.
Retrieve the acid spill kit located outside the door. Do not use water to wash the spill away; adding water to concentrated acid causes an exothermic boiling reaction. Liberally pour soda ash (sodium carbonate) or the provided neutralizer powder directly onto the spill to neutralize the acid. It will fizz violently. Wait until the fizzing stops entirely. Shovel the neutralized slurry into the designated yellow hazmat disposal bins. Wipe the floor with a dilute baking soda solution.
