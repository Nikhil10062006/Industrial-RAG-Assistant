Working at height on the Sinter Plant inclined conveyor gallery roofs. The corrugated metal roofing panels deteriorate rapidly due to the acidic sulfur emissions from the main exhaust stack.
Never step directly onto the corrugated metal roof sheets. They look solid but can be paper-thin from underneath due to acid corrosion, acting as a fatal trapdoor.
Before accessing the roof to repair a hole or clear a blocked gutter, you must install temporary heavy wooden crawling boards (duckboards) that span across the structural steel purlins. You must wear a full-body harness with a twin-tail shock-absorbing lanyard. Tie off 100% of the time to the engineered static lifeline running along the ridge of the roof. Do not tie off to the roof trusses or handrails, as they may also be structurally compromised by corrosion. Always maintain three points of contact when transitioning from the ladder to the crawling boards.
