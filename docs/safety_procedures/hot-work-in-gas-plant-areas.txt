Hot work near coke-oven gas, blast-furnace gas, natural gas, hydrogen, oxygen, or solvent systems requires a permit, gas test, isolation review, fire watch, and control of adjacent openings. The permit is valid only for the area, time, equipment, and conditions stated.

Before striking an arc or lighting a torch, inspect the work boundary, drains, pits, cable trenches, and nearby vessels. Gas can travel away from the visible pipe. Test at different heights and repeat the test during the job. Stop if wind changes, ventilation fails, or the detector alarm rises.

Remove combustible material or shield it. Keep extinguishers and a charged hose available where the assessment requires them. Use gas hoses, regulators, leads, and flashback arrestors in good condition. Do not leave a hot electrode, torch, or welding machine energized during a break.

The fire watch remains for the prescribed period after hot work ends and checks the other side of walls, below floors, and hidden insulation where sparks may have traveled. Close the permit only after the area is inspected and the equipment is left safe.
