Operating area: hydraulic maintenance. Use the approved procedure, identify the equipment and energy state, preserve the first indication, and keep people outside the hazard boundary. An accumulator slowly lost precharge through its gas valve. Isolate, discharge, verify pressure, and inspect valve core and cap using the charging procedure. Recharge only with nitrogen and record as-found and as-left pressure. Record the equipment tag, material or heat identity, time, readings with units, people informed, and the final status. If a temporary control, bypass, manual setting, or alternate route is used, give it an owner and review time. Do not close the event because the alarm cleared or the equipment restarted once. Repeat the relevant test under the same load, speed, temperature, pressure, or material condition that caused the problem. At handover, state what was checked, what remains uncertain, and the exact condition that requires stopping the equipment. Practical plant knowledge is useful when it makes the next safe decision easier without replacing isolation, permit, quality, emergency, or engineering controls.
