A water leak near an electrode arm or roof panel is treated as a molten-metal hazard. Stop power or move to the approved safe state, warn the area, and keep people outside the exclusion zone. Do not approach steam or dripping water to find the source. Check panel flow, pressure, temperature, and leak alarms remotely. Record furnace stage, water circuit, electrode position, and people exposed. Only resume after the water path and electrical insulation are verified. Water and liquid steel must never be allowed to meet through improvisation.  Record the area, equipment, readings, people informed, temporary controls, and clearance authority. Do not rely on a green screen indication when a field check is required. If weather, heat, gas, pressure, movement, visibility, or communication changes, stop and reassess. PPE supports the control but does not replace isolation, barriers, ventilation, or a safe escape route. The handover should state the current operating mode, the last stable reading, the open risk, and the person or discipline responsible for the next check. If a temporary measure is used, give it an owner and review time. Do not leave a condition described only as “normal now”; explain what was observed, what evidence supports the decision, and what change would require stopping the equipment. This is how practical experience becomes usable plant knowledge without weakening the formal control system.
