A suspected water leak near an electric arc furnace roof, panel, or electrode system is an immediate process and explosion hazard. Stop non-essential movement, notify the furnace controller, and follow the approved emergency power and charge-state response. Do not approach a steaming or dripping panel to inspect it. Water contacting molten steel can generate a violent event. Observe from the protected position for steam, spray pattern, panel temperature, electrode behavior, and furnace pressure. Isolate the affected water circuit only when the furnace procedure confirms it will not worsen the condition. Keep people out of the furnace-side exclusion zone and prevent wet scrap or tools from entering. Do not restart a pump or open a drain without understanding where water will discharge. Record the first indication, water pressure, furnace stage, power state, panel identity, and personnel exposure. After the furnace is safe, inspect the panel, hoses, supports, and leak-detection circuit. A small leak found early is still a stop-work signal; it is not an invitation to finish the heat.
