Condensate drains on furnace-gas mains are opened only by authorized personnel under the gas-line procedure. Confirm line identity, pressure, drain pot level, detector status, wind direction, and safe discharge location. Approach from upwind and keep people away from the outlet. Crack the drain slowly from the approved position; a drain pot can contain hot, toxic, or flammable gas as well as liquid. If the outlet hisses continuously, the liquid seal may be lost or the valve may be leaking through. Close the valve and notify the gas controller rather than trying to clear it with a rod. Never discharge condensate into an uncontrolled drain or toward a hot surface. Test the surrounding area at breathing height and in nearby pits. Record drain level, time, gas readings, liquid appearance, valve condition, and any abnormal pressure. A drain that is dry for several rounds can still receive a slug after rain, temperature change, or gas-flow variation. The important control is not merely emptying the pot; it is keeping the gas boundary intact.
