Before lifting billets with a magnet, confirm bundle weight, magnet capacity, bundle integrity, surface condition, and clear travel path. Scale, water, oil, and irregular ends can reduce holding force. Test the magnet on the load at low height and hold briefly before travel. Do not lift a bundle with loose billets protruding or people standing between stacks. Keep the load low and move smoothly; avoid sudden braking and side pull. If the magnet alarm sounds, stop and lower according to the emergency lifting plan. Record magnet ID, load, surface, alarm, and operator. New operators often trust the magnet because it held the previous bundle; each bundle is a separate lift calculation. Chocks and stack stability must be checked before removing the top bundle.

Before starting or restarting, confirm the responsible area owner, the boundary, the communication method, and the emergency route. Record the condition before the intervention and the result after it. Do not rely on a screen indication alone when a field check is possible. If weather, heat, gas, pressure, movement, or visibility changes, stop and reassess rather than continuing on the original assumption. The next shift should receive the equipment tag, time, readings, people informed, temporary controls, and the condition that requires a further stop.
