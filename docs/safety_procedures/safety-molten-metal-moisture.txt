Extreme hazard awareness for moisture in molten metal handling areas (Melt Shop, Cast House). Water expanding into steam increases its volume by a factor of 1600 instantly. When 1500°C liquid steel traps water, the resulting volumetric explosion is catastrophic.
Absolute Rules:

Never charge scrap into the BOF if it has visible ice or trapped water in closed pipes (like old shock absorbers).

During the monsoon, all ladles, tundishes, and scrap chutes must be pre-heated to drive out atmospheric moisture from the refractory pores.

If you spill a water bottle on the cast house deck, you must wipe it completely dry immediately. A single puddle of water trapped under a spilled splash of hot iron will blow the iron straight upward like shrapnel.

Check the cooling water hoses on the oxygen lances. If you see a pinhole leak dripping onto the vessel lip, abort the blow immediately.
