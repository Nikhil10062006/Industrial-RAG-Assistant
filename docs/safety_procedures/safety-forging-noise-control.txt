Rules surrounding the Standard Operating Procedure for Noise Control (SOP-821-036) in the drop-forging and stamping shops. The impact of a 10-ton drop hammer generates peak noise exceeding 130 dB.
Standard foam earplugs are insufficient. Dual-protection is mandatory: Class 5 foam earplugs inserted deeply, covered by heavy-duty structural earmuffs. Audiometric testing is mandatory every 6 months. Communication must rely on bone-conduction headsets or strictly enforced hand signals.
