Asphyxiation hazard protocol for subsurface trenches and valve pits in the Melt Shop.
We use thousands of cubic meters of Argon gas per hour for secondary steel stirring. Argon is 38% heavier than air. It does not dissipate upward. If a leak occurs, the invisible, odorless Argon will flow like water along the floor and pool in the lowest accessible spaces, displacing all oxygen.
Never enter the hydraulic pipe trenches, scale pits, or sub-basements near the Ladle Furnace without a personal multi-gas monitor clipped to your breathing zone (collar, not belt). If your monitor vibrates for low oxygen (< 19.5%), you have roughly 10 seconds of useful consciousness remaining. Hold your breath, turn around, and climb out immediately. Never bend down to pick up a dropped tool in a trench without verifying the atmosphere first.
