Keep a physical barrier and defined exclusion zone around an open slag pit. Check pit level, steam, crust stability, water, crane movement, lighting, and access. Do not walk on slag crust or lean over the edge. If water enters, move away and notify the controller. Record pit, condition, barrier, weather, and work. Black slag can remain liquid or unstable underneath. Hot-metal PPE does not make an unsupported crust safe.  Record the area, equipment, readings, people informed, temporary controls, and clearance authority. Do not rely on a green screen indication when a field check is required. If weather, heat, gas, pressure, movement, visibility, or communication changes, stop and reassess. PPE supports the control but does not replace isolation, barriers, ventilation, or a safe escape route. The handover should state the current operating mode, the last stable reading, the open risk, and the person or discipline responsible for the next check. If a temporary measure is used, give it an owner and review time. Do not leave a condition described only as “normal now”; explain what was observed, what evidence supports the decision, and what change would require stopping the equipment. This is how practical experience becomes usable plant knowledge without weakening the formal control system.
