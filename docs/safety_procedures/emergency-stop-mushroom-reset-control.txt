Emergency-stop buttons are reset only after the hazard is understood, the area is clear, and the equipment owner confirms readiness. Do not reset remotely while someone may be inside a guarded area. Inspect which devices were actuated and preserve the alarm sequence. Record button, equipment, cause, people, and test. Resetting is not a repair. The machine must be restarted in the safest mode and watched for recurrence.  Record the area, equipment, readings, people informed, temporary controls, and clearance authority. Do not rely on a green screen indication when a field check is required. If weather, heat, gas, pressure, movement, visibility, or communication changes, stop and reassess. PPE supports the control but does not replace isolation, barriers, ventilation, or a safe escape route. The handover should state the current operating mode, the last stable reading, the open risk, and the person or discipline responsible for the next check. If a temporary measure is used, give it an owner and review time. Do not leave a condition described only as “normal now”; explain what was observed, what evidence supports the decision, and what change would require stopping the equipment. This is how practical experience becomes usable plant knowledge without weakening the formal control system.
