Before transferring flammable or solvent-containing chemical, verify hose compatibility, bonding and grounding, coupling condition, valve lineup, receiving capacity, and spill containment. Connect the bonding cable before opening the transfer path and confirm continuity through the approved method. Keep ignition sources and vehicles away. Start at low flow and watch for leaks, pressure, hose movement, and tank level. Do not use a hose with damaged braid, exposed reinforcement, or unknown service history. If bonding is lost, stop the transfer and close valves in the approved sequence. Record hose ID, chemical, tank, continuity result, flow, and completion. Static control is a system: correct hose, bonding, flow, ventilation, and ignition-source control must all be present. Never transfer into an unidentified or incompatible container.

Before starting or restarting, confirm the responsible area owner, the boundary, the communication method, and the emergency route. Record the condition before the intervention and the result after it. Do not rely on a screen indication alone when a field check is possible. If weather, heat, gas, pressure, movement, or visibility changes, stop and reassess rather than continuing on the original assumption. The next shift should receive the equipment tag, time, readings, people informed, temporary controls, and the condition that requires a further stop.
