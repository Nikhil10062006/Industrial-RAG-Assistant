Maintenance safety protocol for oxygen network valves and piping (BOF, Caster lancing stations).
Pure oxygen under pressure is aggressively reactive. Any organic material—even a single fingerprint of lubricating grease—will auto-ignite due to the heat of adiabatic compression when an oxygen valve is opened quickly.
Strict Rule: No standard oils, greases, or hydrocarbon-based solvents are permitted within 10 meters of an open oxygen line repair.
All oxygen valves and gaskets must be ordered "Cleaned for Oxygen Service" (sealed in plastic bags). Technicians must wash their hands and don brand-new, clean cotton gloves before handling these parts. If a threaded fitting requires sealant, use only specified, oxygen-compatible PTFE tape. Open all oxygen valves extremely slowly; a fast opening creates a supersonic shockwave that heats the gas enough to ignite any microscopic metal burrs inside the pipe, causing a titanium-white metal fire that will burn straight through the steel pipe wall.
