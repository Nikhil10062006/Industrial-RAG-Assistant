Emergency protocol for an Anhydrous Ammonia leak in the Coke By-Products (CBP) plant. Ammonia is highly corrosive, toxic, and forms explosive mixtures.
If the ammonia leak detection system triggers a high alarm (>50 ppm), the CBP unit must be evacuated upwind immediately. Note the windsock directions mounted on the exhauster building roof. Do not enter the ammonia scrubber area without a Level A fully encapsulating chemical suit and SCBA.
If a leak is identified at a flange or pump seal, the fire brigade must deploy water fog monitors (wide spray) immediately. Ammonia gas is highly soluble in water; a heavy water fog will knock the vapor cloud out of the air and prevent it from drifting into adjacent communities. Do not shoot a straight water stream directly at a liquid ammonia leak, as the heat of the water will accelerate boiling and gas generation. All contaminated runoff water must be diverted to the emergency containment basin.
