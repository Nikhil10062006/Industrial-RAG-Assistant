Safety guidelines for executing the Standard Operating Procedure for Magnetic Particle Testing (SOP-821-012) in the QA lab. This test checks for microscopic surface cracks by inducing a heavy magnetic field.
The electromagnetic yokes draw extremely high current. You must stand on rubber dielectric mats. Never trigger the yoke while freely suspended in the air; it will overheat the coil. Ensure the contact pads are firmly seated on the steel before energizing. The fluorescent bath contains aerosolized hydrocarbons—the local exhaust ventilation hood must be running, and technicians must wear a half-mask organic vapor respirator.
