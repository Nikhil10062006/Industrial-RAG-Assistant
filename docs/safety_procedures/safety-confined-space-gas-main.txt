Safety protocols for Confined Space Entry into a Blast Furnace Gas (BFG) main. This is the highest-risk confined space in the plant.
The pipe must be positively isolated with physical slip-blinds, not just valves. The main must be purged with Nitrogen, then forced-ventilated with fresh air blowers for 24 hours. The standby person must continuously monitor the exhaust air for CO. Entry is strictly prohibited if CO is above 30 ppm or Oxygen is below 19.5%. All entrants must wear full-body harnesses attached to a retrieval winch, and must carry an emergency 5-minute escape air cylinder.
