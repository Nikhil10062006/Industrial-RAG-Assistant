Lockout-Tagout (LOTO) procedure for isolating an Overhead EOT Crane prior to maintenance on the bridge or trolley. You cannot rely solely on the crane cabin's main isolator switch.

The crane operator must park the crane at the designated maintenance platform access stairs.

The operator switches off the cabin isolator.

The maintenance technician must climb to the primary bridge walkway and locate the main incoming power collector shoes (the sliders that touch the live Down Shop Leads).

Throw the physical heavy-duty disconnect switch located immediately adjacent to the collector shoes.

Apply your personal padlock and LOTO tag to the disconnect handle.

Using a certified high-voltage multimeter, test across all three phases (L1-L2, L2-L3, L1-L3) and to ground on the load side of the switch to verify zero voltage.
Never bypass this step; adjacent cranes can sometimes induce lethal voltages into the rails, or a bridging fault can backfeed the panel.
