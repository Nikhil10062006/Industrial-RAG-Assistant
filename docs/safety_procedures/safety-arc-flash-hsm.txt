Arc flash safety boundaries in the Hot Strip Mill (HSM) 11kV electrical basement. The massive synchronus motors driving the mill stands are fed by massive switchgear capable of delivering a 50kA short-circuit fault current.
If an arc flash occurs in this switchgear, the copper busbars instantly vaporize, expanding 67,000 times in volume and generating a 20,000°C plasma fireball.
Enforcement: The switchgear room is a restricted zone. No personnel may enter the room while the mill is actively rolling without wearing full Category 2 Arc Flash PPE (minimum 8 cal/cm2), which includes an FR shirt, FR pants, safety glasses, and a hard hat. If you are actively racking a breaker in or out, you must wear the Category 4 suit (40 cal/cm2) with the full hood. Never lean against the switchgear panel doors. If a fault happens, those doors will blow off their hinges with the force of a bomb.
