Heat Stress monitoring and mandatory work-rest cycles on the Blast Furnace Cast House floor. Radiant heat from the molten iron runners can push localized ambient temperatures above 60°C.
We utilize the Wet Bulb Globe Temperature (WBGT) index, which accounts for ambient temp, humidity, wind, and radiant heat. The digital WBGT monitors are mounted on pillar 4 and 6.
If the WBGT reads above 32°C (Category 4 / Red Flag): Continuous heavy labor is strictly prohibited. Work cycles must immediately shift to 20 minutes of active work followed by 40 minutes of rest in the air-conditioned cooling cabins. Hydration with electrolyte replacement fluids is mandatory; plain water is insufficient and can lead to hyponatremia (water intoxication) when sweating heavily. Supervisors must enforce the buddy system and actively look for signs of heat exhaustion: sudden cessation of sweating, confusion, and extreme fatigue.
