Emergency Stop (E-Stop) actuation and reset procedure for the Sinter Plant Primary Crusher and associated conveyors. E-stop pull cords run the entire length of the conveyor galleries.
Any worker who identifies a severe hazard (e.g., personnel entangled, major mechanical failure, severe fire) is authorized to pull the E-stop cord. Activating the cord immediately drops the power to the crusher and feed belts.
Crucially, never reset an E-stop blindly. If a cord is pulled, the control room will radio the location. A supervisor must physically walk the entire length of the tripped conveyor and visually inspect the crusher throat to ensure all personnel are clear and the hazard is resolved. Only after visual confirmation of the entire line can the local reset button on the E-stop switch be engaged, followed by verbal clearance to the pulpit to restart the sequence.
