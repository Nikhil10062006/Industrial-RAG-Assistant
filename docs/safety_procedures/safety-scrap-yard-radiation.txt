Protocol for radiation detection in the Steel Melting Shop Scrap Yard. Incoming railcars of recycled steel scrap occasionally contain "orphaned" radioactive sources (e.g., discarded medical or industrial Cobalt-60 or Cesium-137 devices). If melted in our furnace, it will contaminate the entire plant's dust extraction system, costing tens of millions of dollars to clean up.
All incoming scrap railcars and trucks MUST pass slowly through the portal radiation monitors at the weighbridge at a maximum speed of 5 km/h.
If the portal alarm triggers: The vehicle must be stopped immediately. Do not approach the load. The security and environmental team must be notified. A trained radiation safety officer will approach the vehicle using a handheld Geiger-Muller counter and a telescoping survey meter to pinpoint the source hotspot. Under no circumstances is the load to be tipped in the yard. The vehicle will be quarantined in the designated lead-shielded containment bay pending state regulatory intervention.
