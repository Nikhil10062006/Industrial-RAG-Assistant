For a strip break near the zinc pot, stop the line and follow the pot emergency procedure. Keep people away from the strip, sink roll, and splash zone. Do not reach for the loose strip or move a roll without isolation. Control line speed, pot equipment, and crane traffic from the approved station. Record strip grade, speed, tension, pot temperature, break location, and recovery. A broken strip may recoil or pull equipment unexpectedly. Inspect sink roll, air knife, guides, and strip path before rethreading.  Record the area, equipment, readings, people informed, temporary controls, and clearance authority. Do not rely on a green screen indication when a field check is required. If weather, heat, gas, pressure, movement, visibility, or communication changes, stop and reassess. PPE supports the control but does not replace isolation, barriers, ventilation, or a safe escape route. The handover should state the current operating mode, the last stable reading, the open risk, and the person or discipline responsible for the next check. If a temporary measure is used, give it an owner and review time. Do not leave a condition described only as “normal now”; explain what was observed, what evidence supports the decision, and what change would require stopping the equipment. This is how practical experience becomes usable plant knowledge without weakening the formal control system.
