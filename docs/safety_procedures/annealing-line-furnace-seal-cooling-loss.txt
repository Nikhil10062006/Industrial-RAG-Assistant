If a furnace-seal cooling circuit loses flow, reduce or stop the line according to the atmosphere procedure and keep people away from hot seals. Check pump, pressure, temperature, valve position, and leak detection remotely. Do not open the seal housing while the furnace is hot or under atmosphere. Record zone, water readings, furnace pressure, atmosphere, and response. A seal failure can admit air, damage equipment, and create a fire risk. Restore flow and verify atmosphere before restart.  Record the area, equipment, readings, people informed, temporary controls, and clearance authority. Do not rely on a green screen indication when a field check is required. If weather, heat, gas, pressure, movement, visibility, or communication changes, stop and reassess. PPE supports the control but does not replace isolation, barriers, ventilation, or a safe escape route. The handover should state the current operating mode, the last stable reading, the open risk, and the person or discipline responsible for the next check. If a temporary measure is used, give it an owner and review time. Do not leave a condition described only as “normal now”; explain what was observed, what evidence supports the decision, and what change would require stopping the equipment. This is how practical experience becomes usable plant knowledge without weakening the formal control system.
