Emergency response for a ruptured Hydrochloric Acid (HCl) pipe in the Cold Mill Pickling Line. HCl is highly corrosive and emits choking, acidic fumes that burn the respiratory tract.
If a pressurized line ruptures, hit the nearest emergency pump stop button immediately to kill the flow. Evacuate the immediate area and approach strictly from upwind.
Do not attempt to wash a concentrated acid spill down the drain with standard water hoses. HCl reacts exothermically with water and will create a massive cloud of toxic steam. You must isolate the area and deploy the limestone (calcium carbonate) neutralizer powder from the yellow spill bins. Cover the entire acid puddle with the powder. It will fizz violently as it turns the acid into harmless calcium chloride salt and CO2 gas. Once the fizzing stops completely, the neutralized slurry can be safely shoveled into hazmat bins and the floor flushed with a weak alkaline solution.
