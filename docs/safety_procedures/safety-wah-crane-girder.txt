Safety procedure for Working at Height (WAH) on Overhead Electric Traveling (EOT) Crane girders. This applies to all mechanical and electrical track maintenance.
Prior to accessing the crane stairway, the crane must be parked at the designated maintenance platform. The primary power busbars (Down Shop Leads) must be de-energized, locked out, and tagged out at the main substation. You must physically verify the busbars are dead using a rated high-voltage proximity tester before stepping onto the girder. 100% tie-off is mandatory. A full-body harness with a double-lanyard is required. You must be clipped to the engineered static lifeline running parallel to the tracks at all times; never clip onto handrails or conduit. A standby person must remain on the ground with a radio. Drop zones must be barricaded with red danger tape to protect floor workers from falling tools. All tools must be tethered to your tool belt.
