Protocol for accessing the roof of the Blast Furnace Gas (BFG) Holder (100,000 cubic meter capacity). The gas holder roof floats on a cushion of BFG.
Accessing the roof while the holder is in service is an extreme hazard task, permitted only for mandatory safety valve inspections. A minimum of three personnel is required: two entrants and one spotter on the external staircase. All personnel must wear full-face supplied-air respirators (airline systems) connected to a clean air cascade cart at ground level. Personal CO monitors are mandatory but will likely max out instantly if there is a seal leak.
Never step off the designated steel tread-plate pathways onto the thin membrane seal. Tools must be 100% brass or beryllium-copper to prevent sparks. Mobile phones and standard radios are strictly prohibited; only intrinsically safe (ATEX Zone 0 rated) communication devices are allowed. Maximum time on the roof is 15 minutes.
