Handling concentrated Sulfuric Acid (98%) and Sodium Hypochlorite (Bleach) at the plant water treatment facility.
Never allow acid and bleach to mix; the reaction instantly releases lethal Chlorine gas. Ensure the fill ports for the tanks are physically keyed differently and color-coded. When connecting a tanker truck for offloading, visually verify the bill of lading and physically unlock the specific cam-lock fitting. The operator must wear a Level B chemical suit, acid-proof apron, face shield, and heavy PVC gloves.
