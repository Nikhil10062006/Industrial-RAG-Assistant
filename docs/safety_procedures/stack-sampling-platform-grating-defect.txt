Do not access a stack sampling platform with missing grating, damaged handrail, poor lighting, or an unverified anchor. Barricade the access and report the defect. Sampling equipment must not be carried up an unsafe route. Record platform, defect, weather, and temporary control. A compliance sample is not more important than fall protection. Repair and inspect the platform before resuming.  Record the area, equipment, readings, people informed, temporary controls, and clearance authority. Do not rely on a green screen indication when a field check is required. If weather, heat, gas, pressure, movement, visibility, or communication changes, stop and reassess. PPE supports the control but does not replace isolation, barriers, ventilation, or a safe escape route. The handover should state the current operating mode, the last stable reading, the open risk, and the person or discipline responsible for the next check. If a temporary measure is used, give it an owner and review time. Do not leave a condition described only as “normal now”; explain what was observed, what evidence supports the decision, and what change would require stopping the equipment. This is how practical experience becomes usable plant knowledge without weakening the formal control system.
