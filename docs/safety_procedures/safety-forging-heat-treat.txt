Safety protocols during operations covered by the Standard Operating Procedure for Heat Treatment Process (SOP-821-002). Batch furnaces operate at up to 950°C and utilize controlled, potentially explosive atmospheres.
Never open the furnace door without verifying that the flame curtain at the threshold has ignited to burn off combustible gases. When executing the Standard Operating Procedure for Cooling and Quenching (SOP-821-007), ensure personnel stand strictly behind blast shields. Plunging a 900°C forging into a quench tank generates massive flames. Verify agitation pumps are running; stagnant oil will boil, creating a vapor blanket that can ignite a tank fire.
