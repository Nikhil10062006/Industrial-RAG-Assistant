Operating area: Yard, warehouse, rail, truck, cranes, and material handling. Keep people out of roll, drop, swing, pinch, and vehicle blind zones. Stop when route, load, or communication changes. A rail wagon brake cylinder did not release after washdown. Test pressure, hoses, cylinders, handbrake, and wagon movement before release. The wagon was not moved on a verbal assurance that brakes were fine. Record the equipment tag, material or heat identity, time, readings with units, people informed, and the final status. If a temporary control, bypass, manual setting, or alternate route is used, give it an owner and review time. Do not close the event because the alarm cleared or the equipment restarted once. Repeat the relevant test under the same load, speed, temperature, pressure, or material condition that caused the problem. At handover, state what was checked, what remains uncertain, and the exact condition that requires stopping the equipment. Practical plant knowledge is useful when it makes the next safe decision easier without replacing isolation, permit, quality, emergency, or engineering controls.
