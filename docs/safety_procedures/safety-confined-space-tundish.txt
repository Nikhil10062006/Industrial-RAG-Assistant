Protocol for entering a Continuous Caster Tundish for refractory deskulling and inspection. Even when drained, a tundish is an extreme confined space hazard.
A tundish must sit on the cooling stands for a minimum of 12 hours with forced air fans blowing inside. Before the permit is signed, a physical barricade must be locked over the Argon stirring supply lines. Argon is heavily used in the tundish; if a valve leaks, the heavy gas will pool in the bottom of the tundish bowl, creating an invisible, odorless death trap.
The safety watcher must lower the gas detector probe completely to the floor of the tundish near the nozzle wells (the lowest point) before clearing it for entry. If residual heat inside the tundish exceeds 45°C, entrants must wear ice-vest cooling suits, and stay-times are strictly limited to 15-minute intervals. Do not attempt to use pneumatic jackhammers on the refractory if the temperature is above 60°C, as the vibration paired with heat stress will cause rapid operator collapse.
