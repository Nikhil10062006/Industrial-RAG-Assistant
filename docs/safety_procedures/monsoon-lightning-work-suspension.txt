Suspend outdoor lifting, roof work, crane maintenance, tall-structure access, and exposed electrical work when lightning or the site weather warning reaches the defined threshold. Move people to the designated shelter, lower suspended loads safely, and secure loose material. Do not shelter under conveyors, tanks, cranes, or isolated trees. Record time of suspension, work location, load state, and restart condition. Lightning can strike before rain arrives and can induce voltage on structures and cables. Restart only after the responsible authority releases the area and checks water, wind, visibility, and equipment. Production pressure does not override a weather hold. Include contractors and vehicle operators in the notification.

Before starting or restarting, confirm the responsible area owner, the boundary, the communication method, and the emergency route. Record the condition before the intervention and the result after it. Do not rely on a screen indication alone when a field check is possible. If weather, heat, gas, pressure, movement, or visibility changes, stop and reassess rather than continuing on the original assumption. The next shift should receive the equipment tag, time, readings, people informed, temporary controls, and the condition that requires a further stop.
