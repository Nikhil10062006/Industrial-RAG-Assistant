Safety exclusions and protocols for Slag Pot Handling and transport. Slag pots carry liquid furnace slag at 1500°C. Slag is highly reactive with water.
When the Kress carrier (slag pot transporter) is moving, a strict 15-meter radial exclusion zone applies. All pedestrian and vehicular traffic must yield and clear the area. The ground along the transport route and in the slag dumping yard must be bone dry. If there is standing water in the slag yard, dumping is strictly prohibited. Pouring liquid slag onto a puddle will cause a catastrophic volumetric steam explosion, capable of throwing molten rock hundreds of meters. During monsoons, slag must be allowed to crust over in the pot for 12 hours before dumping. The carrier operator must remain in the armored cab during the tipping process and ensure the windshield blast shields are lowered.
