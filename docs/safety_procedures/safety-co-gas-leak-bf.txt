Emergency response protocol for a Carbon Monoxide (CO) gas leak in the Blast Furnace area. CO is colorless, odorless, and highly toxic. All personnel in the BF area must wear personal CO monitors at all times.
If your monitor alarms at the Low Level (50 ppm): Immediately stop work, don your half-mask respirator, and move to a cross-draft area. Inform the shift supervisor.
If the High Level alarm sounds (100 ppm) or the plant-wide sirens activate: This is a lethal environment. Evacuate immediately via the designated upwind escape routes. Do not attempt to find the source of the leak or close valves without self-contained breathing apparatus (SCBA). Only the designated Gas Rescue Team, fully equipped with SCBA, is permitted to enter a red zone to perform isolation. If a colleague collapses, do not attempt a rescue without SCBA; you will become the second victim. Use the emergency pull-stations to alert the rescue team.
