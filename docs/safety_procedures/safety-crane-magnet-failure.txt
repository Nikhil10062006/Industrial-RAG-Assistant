Emergency procedures for Overhead Electric Traveling (EOT) cranes operating heavy-duty lifting electromagnets in the Scrap Yard and Billet Yard.
A power grid failure will instantly drop any scrap or steel billets attached to the magnet, posing a massive crushing hazard. To mitigate this, all magnet cranes are equipped with UPS battery backup banks on the bridge.
If the main plant power fails, the backup battery will automatically engage to maintain magnetic flux. You have exactly 10 minutes of battery life.
When the power fails and the siren sounds, the crane operator must immediately stop all bridge and trolley motion. Do not try to move the load to a "better" spot. Lower the main hoist immediately and set the load down exactly where it is, even if it's in the middle of a driveway or walkway. Once the load is firmly on the ground, switch off the magnet power to save the batteries. Ground personnel must never walk under a magnetized load, even for a second.
