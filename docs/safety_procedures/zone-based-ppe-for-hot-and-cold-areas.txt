PPE is selected by the hazard of the task, not by the job title. In a general production area, wear helmet with chin strap where required, safety glasses, safety shoes, hearing protection in marked zones, flame-resistant clothing where hot work or hot metal is present, and gloves suited to the material. Ordinary cotton gloves are not a substitute for cut or heat protection.

Near molten metal, use the designated aluminized or heat-rated clothing, face shield, gloves, spats, and metatarsal protection. Keep synthetic fabric away from radiant heat. Near chemical dosing or water-treatment areas, use the chemical goggles, face shield, suit, and gloves listed on the safety data sheet. Near oxygen equipment, use clean, oil-free clothing and tools.

For gas testing, the detector is not PPE. It is a control measure. Respiratory protection, if required, must be selected and fit-tested for the contaminant. Do not wear a filter mask in an oxygen-deficient atmosphere or for carbon monoxide unless the site respiratory program specifically permits it.

Inspect PPE before use. Replace cracked face shields, heat-damaged sleeves, contaminated gloves, and shoes with damaged soles. If the PPE makes the job difficult, stop and obtain the correct item; workers often remove uncomfortable equipment just when exposure increases.
