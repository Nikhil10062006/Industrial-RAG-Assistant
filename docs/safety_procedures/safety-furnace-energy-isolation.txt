Energy Isolation and Lockout-Tagout (LOTO) rules for Blast Furnace maintenance. In steel plants, the complexity is compounded by interconnected systems. You cannot just isolate the local equipment. For example, a blast furnace requires isolating not just the furnace itself but also the stove system that feeds it hot blast air.
If entering the bustle pipe, the isolation must physically lock out the cold blast main, the stove gas burners, and the pulverized coal injection system. Relying purely on automated butterfly valves is forbidden; use physical mechanical slip-blinds to guarantee zero hazardous energy.
