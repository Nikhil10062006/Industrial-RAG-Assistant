Manual backwash sequence for the Hot Strip Mill primary sand filters. These filters strip heavy iron oxide scale and oil from the cooling water before it goes to the cooling towers.
When the differential pressure across the filter bed exceeds 0.8 bar, a backwash is required. Isolate the filter by closing the primary inlet and outlet butterfly valves. Open the backwash drain valve to the sludge thickener.
Start the air-scour blower first. Run the air scour for exactly 5 minutes. This breaks up the crusted oil and scale on top of the sand. Crucial: Do not start the backwash water pump while the air blower is running. If you run air and water simultaneously, you will blow the expensive filter media (garnet and sand) straight out the drain pipe. Shut off the air, wait 60 seconds for the bed to settle, and then start the backwash water pump. Run water for 10 minutes until the sight glass runs clear.
