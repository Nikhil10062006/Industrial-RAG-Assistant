Operational protocol for the High-Pressure Descaler headers immediately exiting the Walking Beam Reheat Furnace in the Hot Strip Mill. Slabs exit the furnace at 1200°C covered in a thick layer of primary iron oxide scale. The descaler uses 160-bar water jets to blast this scale off before the slab hits the roughing mill.
The descaling pumps must be brought up to pressure (minimum 150 bar) before the furnace discharge door opens. The automatic optical sensors will trigger the water valves as the slab approaches.
Crucial constraint: Do not ever override the descaler valves to "manual ON" if a slab is stationary on the roller table. If a 1200°C slab sits under the 160-bar water jets for more than 5 seconds, you will create a massive "cold spot" in the steel. When that cold spot hits the roughing mill rolls, it will either shatter the work rolls or trip the 10MW mill drive on over-torque. If a slab stalls under the headers, the water must be shut off instantly.
