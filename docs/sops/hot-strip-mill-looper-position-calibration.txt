Before calibrating a looper position sensor, confirm the strip path is empty, the looper is mechanically free, and hydraulic or pneumatic energy is controlled. Inspect the arm, roller, stops, cylinder, feedback linkage, and cable. Use the approved reference positions and do not climb onto the looper. Compare the mechanical angle with the screen value at low, mid, and high positions. If the signal is nonlinear, inspect linkage geometry and sensor mounting before changing scaling. Run the looper through the approved stroke with guards in place and no strip. Record reference angle, indicated angle, sensor serial number, linkage condition, and loop response. A looper that reaches its limit during rolling can look like a tension problem; calibration and mechanical travel must both be correct. After calibration, thread at low speed and verify the actual angle under tension. Do not leave an override active after the test.

Record the equipment tag, operating condition, readings with units, and person who accepted the result. Compare the actual field condition with the control-room indication and the approved drawing or job card. If any value is outside the normal band, hold the task and obtain the responsible specialist rather than making an undocumented adjustment. At handover, state what was checked, what was changed, what remains open, and the exact condition that would require a stop.
