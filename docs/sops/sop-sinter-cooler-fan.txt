Startup sequence for the Sinter Plant Annular Cooler forced-draft fans. These fans blow ambient air up through the hot sinter to cool it from 600°C down to 100°C before it hits the rubber conveyor belts.
Before starting Fan #1, ensure the cooler louver dampers are set to 5% open to minimize motor starting current. The 11kV motor draws a massive inrush current; if you start it with open dampers, the breaker will trip on overcurrent.
Start the lube oil pump and verify bearing pressure is above 1.5 bar. Initiate the main motor start. Once the motor reaches synchronous speed (1480 RPM), watch the ammeter. Slowly modulate the louver dampers open to 60%. If the fan vibrates heavily, check the fan casing drain valves. Condensation and dust often form a heavy mud inside the casing that throws the impeller out of balance. Drain the mud, hose out the casing, and restart.
