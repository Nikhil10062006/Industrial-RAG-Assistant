Procedure utilizing the Standard Operating Procedure for Material Inspection and Acceptance (SOP-821-001) in the forging shop. Raw billet quality dictates final forged product integrity. Before charging billets into the reheat furnace, inspect the batch for surface seams, deep scratches, or piping defects. Verify the heat number stamped on the billets against the metallurgical lab certificate. If carbon or alloy content deviates by more than 0.05% from the target spec, quarantine the batch. We also strictly rely on the Standard Operating Procedure for Ultrasonic Testing (SOP-821-011) to detect internal voids. Never bypass the ultrasonic check on critical automotive grades.
