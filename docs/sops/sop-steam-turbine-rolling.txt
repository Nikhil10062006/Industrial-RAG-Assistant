Cold start "rolling" procedure for the Captive Power Plant 30 MW Steam Turbine. Turbine rotors sag slightly under their own weight when completely stationary.
Before admitting any steam, the turbine must be on the electric turning gear (barring gear) at 150 RPM for a minimum of 12 hours. Verify the shaft eccentricity on the Bently Nevada rack is below 0.05mm. If eccentricity is high, keep it on barring until the rotor straightens.
Once cleared, pull main condenser vacuum to -0.85 bar. Crack the bypass steam valve to roll the turbine off the barring gear. Hold at 600 RPM for 30 minutes to ensure uniform thermal expansion (the "heat soak"). During the ramp to the synchronous speed of 3000 RPM, you will pass through the critical harmonic speed (around 1850 RPM). Do not linger at critical speed. Push through it rapidly to prevent severe resonance and bearing damage.
