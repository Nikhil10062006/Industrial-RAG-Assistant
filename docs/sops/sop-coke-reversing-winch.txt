SOP for manual operation of the Coke Oven Battery heating reversing winch. The reversing winch flips the combustion air and waste gas flows every 20 minutes to maintain even battery temperatures. If the automated PLC sequence fails, the shift technician must perform the reversal manually within a 3-minute window to avoid battery cooling and pressure loss.
Switch the winch panel to LOCAL. Disengage the automatic clutch. Sound the warning horn for 10 seconds. Manually drive the winch forward to close the air valves on the coke side. You must physically watch the tension cable—if it goes slack, a valve is jammed. Do not overdrive the winch motor or you will snap the main cable. Once the midpoint is reached, wait exactly 10 seconds for the flues to purge waste gas. Then, continue driving the winch to open the pusher side air valves. Verify all mechanical indicator flags on the alleyway tie-rods are pointing fully to the "A" position. Re-engage the auto clutch once the fault is cleared.
