Procedure for taking a manual dip temperature and chemistry sample from the Basic Oxygen Furnace (BOF) after the main blow.
The vessel must be tilted down to exactly 85 degrees (sampling position). The pulpit operator must confirm the tilt brake is locked. The sampler must wear full primary and secondary aluminized PPE, including the gold-tinted face shield. Attach the disposable cardboard thermocouple/sampler probe to the steel lance.
Approach the vessel lip cautiously. Do not stand directly in front of the mouth; stand to the drive side to avoid sudden slag splashing. Plunge the probe deeply through the slag layer directly into the liquid steel bath. If you don't punch through the slag, your chemistry sample will be contaminated with oxides and useless to the lab. Hold the probe steady for exactly 5 seconds until the green light flashes on the local display panel indicating a successful temp read. Withdraw, knock the steel sample cup out on the deck, and send it via the pneumatic tube to the spectrometer lab.
