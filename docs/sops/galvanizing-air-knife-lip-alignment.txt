Before aligning an air-knife lip, confirm strip position, pot condition, line speed, air pressure, knife temperature, and access barriers. Adjustment tools must be secured and kept away from the moving strip. Use the approved feeler or optical method; do not touch the lip or strip by hand. Measure the gap across the width and compare the knife parallelism with the strip. A small local obstruction can create coating-thickness variation or streaks. If air pressure or flow is unstable, correct the supply before changing the lip. Record knife number, gap, pressure, strip width, speed, and coating result. Veteran note: a coating profile that changes with strip width often points to alignment or flow distribution, not only bath chemistry. After adjustment, produce a controlled trial and hold it for quality review before returning to full speed.

Record the equipment tag, operating condition, readings with units, and person who accepted the result. Compare the actual field condition with the control-room indication and the approved drawing or job card. If any value is outside the normal band, hold the task and obtain the responsible specialist rather than making an undocumented adjustment. At handover, state what was checked, what was changed, what remains open, and the exact condition that would require a stop.
