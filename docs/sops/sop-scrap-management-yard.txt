Execution of the Standard Operating Procedure for Scrap Management (SOP-821-033) in the Melt Shop yard. Direct incoming trucks to their designated bays: Heavy Melting Scrap (HMS) to Bay 1, Shredded to Bay 2, and Light scrap to Bay 3. The crane operator must use the electro-magnet to spread out piles of shredded scrap for a visual inspection. If sealed containers or heavy oil are found, reject the load. When building a scrap charge bucket, place light scrap at the very bottom to cushion the fall, and place the heavy HMS on top to push the light scrap deep into the liquid iron bath.
