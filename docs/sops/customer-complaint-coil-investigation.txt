When a customer complaint arrives for a coil or plate, preserve the material, records, samples, process trends, inspection images, and shipment information. Do not alter, rework, or relabel the material before quality defines the investigation plan. Confirm heat, coil, grade, dimensions, defect location, customer process, and photographs. Compare the complaint pattern with roll pitch, weld location, coil head or tail, heat sequence, and inspection coverage. Record containment, affected range, retained samples, and responsible investigator. Veteran quality staff avoid deciding cause from one photograph. Verify whether the defect can be reproduced, whether it was present before shipment, and whether the customer’s measurement method is comparable. Findings must distinguish confirmed cause, contributing condition, and unknowns. Corrective action should change a control or process, not only remind operators to be careful.

Record the equipment tag, operating condition, readings with units, and person who accepted the result. Compare the actual field condition with the control-room indication and the approved drawing or job card. If any value is outside the normal band, hold the task and obtain the responsible specialist rather than making an undocumented adjustment. At handover, state what was checked, what was changed, what remains open, and the exact condition that would require a stop.
