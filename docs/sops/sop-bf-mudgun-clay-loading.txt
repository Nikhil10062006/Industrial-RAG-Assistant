Procedure for loading the Blast Furnace hydraulic mudgun with taphole clay. The consistency of the resin-bonded clay is critical for a safe taphole closure.
During the winter months (ambient < 15°C), the clay blocks will become completely rigid. Do not attempt to load cold clay into the mudgun barrel; the extrusion pressure will spike above 250 bar and rupture the hydraulic seals. Cold clay must be pre-heated in the cast house conditioning oven to 45°C for 24 hours prior to use.
Conversely, during peak summer, store the clay in the shaded bins. If it gets too hot, the resin binder cures prematurely inside the barrel, turning into a concrete-like plug that takes two shifts to drill out manually. When loading, clean out the old residual clay from the gun throat using a pneumatic spade. Push the new blocks in firmly, ensuring no air pockets are trapped. An air pocket will cause a sudden pressure drop during plugging, resulting in a dangerous hot metal splashback on the cast house floor.
