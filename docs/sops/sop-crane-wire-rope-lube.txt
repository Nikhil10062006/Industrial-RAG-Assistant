Lubrication protocol for Overhead Crane hoist wire ropes. Improper lubrication is the leading cause of internal wire rope corrosion and sudden snapping.
Never coat a wire rope in thick, tacky, black bearing grease. Thick grease simply coats the outside of the rope, trapping moisture inside the core, and worse, it hides broken outer wire strands from visual inspection.
Use only the specified lightweight, penetrating wire rope oil (e.g., ROCOL). Application must be done while the rope is passing over the sheave drum, as this is when the wire strands spread apart, allowing the oil to penetrate down to the sisal or steel core. If you find more than 3 broken strands in a single rope lay (one complete twist), halt lifting operations and call the rigger shop for a full rope change.
