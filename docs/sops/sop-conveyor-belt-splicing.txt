Procedure for hot vulcanized splicing of heavy-duty steel cord conveyor belts. Strip the rubber covers back exactly 1500mm on both ends, exposing the steel cords. Ensure absolutely no oil or grease touches the bare cords. Stagger the cords precisely according to the joint pattern map. Apply the unvulcanized core rubber and cover rubber. Position the heavy vulcanizing press over the joint. Heat the press to 145°C and apply 14 bar of hydraulic pressure. Hold the cure time for exactly 60 minutes, and allow it to cool below 60°C before releasing pressure.
