Manual alignment procedure for the Coke Oven Pusher Machine when optical positioning sensors fail. Misaligning the pusher ram with the oven will result in pushing the brick wall down, causing a $5 million catastrophic failure.
Switch the traction drive to local manual mode. Drive the machine at creeping speed (10% joystick) toward the target oven. The operator must step out of the cabin onto the spotting platform.
Visually align the heavy steel indicator arrow on the pusher chassis with the painted centerline mark on the battery buckstay. Once aligned, drop the physical track chocks (locking pins) to secure the machine to the rails. You must then perform a "dry run" of the ram head. Jog the ram forward 1 meter to verify it enters the oven door frame perfectly dead-center, with equal clearance on the left and right brickwork. Only after visual confirmation of the gap should you initiate the full push cycle.
