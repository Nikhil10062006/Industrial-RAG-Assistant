Procedure for the physical isolation of a Blast Furnace Gas (BFG) main prior to maintenance. Relying solely on butterfly valves for BFG isolation is strictly prohibited due to high leakage rates and the extreme CO content (up to 25%). Isolation requires filling the U-seal and inserting a mechanical blanking plate.
First, coordinate with the gas dispatcher to ensure line pressure is below 1500 mmWC. Open the water supply to the U-seal. Verify water flows out of the upper overflow tell-tale pipe, indicating the seal is full and holding water. Only then can pipefitters approach the flange to insert the blank (spectacle blind). Fitters must wear supplied-air respirators—no exceptions. Loosen the flange bolts and crack the joint. Sniff the gap with a portable CO monitor. If CO is >50ppm, the U-seal is passing gas; abort the blanking and troubleshoot the seal. Once the blank is fully bolted in, open the downstream purge valves and flush the isolated section with nitrogen until CO is verified at 0ppm.
