Shift startup sequence for the RMHS Control Room. Take charge from the previous shift by checking the logbook, walky-talky, and taking relevant information on supply, maintenance, or breakdowns. Check all bin levels and review any interlock faults like ZSS (Zero Speed Switch), BSS (Belt Sway Switch), or Overload. First, check the path condition in the HMI by clicking all paths one by one. If any equipment icon is displayed in red, it means it is not ready to start; first, remove the fault and make it ready before selecting it for operation. Do not reset equipment in red without consulting maintenance agencies, as it may cause a big breakdown. Start the group with the critical information related to bin levels in mind.
