Before the roll change, confirm the work-roll and backup-roll identifiers, diameter, crown, surface finish, and planned stand position. Check the chock, bearings, seals, cooling passages, and roll-neck condition. Clean the seating surfaces; a small chip can create roll skew or vibration.

After inserting the roll, verify chock seating, roll-gap zero, roll-bending connection, coolant header connection, encoder coupling, and guard position. Perform the approved slow rotation check. Listen for rubbing and watch for coolant leaks at the swivel joints.

Run the stand unloaded and record the unloaded force, roll speed feedback, vibration, and gap response. If zero force or gap differs from the expected band, stop before threading strip. A new roll can be dimensionally correct and still be wrongly assigned to the stand.

Enter the actual roll diameter and roll number into the control system, then have a second person compare the screen with the roll card. Record any roll change delay, defect, and correction. Never assume the previous stand data is still valid after a roll change.
