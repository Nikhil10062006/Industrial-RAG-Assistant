Weekly flushing protocol for the Hot Strip Mill Finishing Stand roll cooling headers. Dirty river water causes silt buildup inside the spray headers, choking the nozzles. A choked nozzle creates a hot band on the work roll, which expands thermally and prints a "wavy edge" defect onto the steel strip.
During the weekly down-day, isolate the main water supply to Stand F3. Remove the end-caps on both the top and bottom cooling headers. Manually open the high-pressure flush valve to blast the stagnant silt out of the pipes. You must visually inspect the discharge; run the flush until the water turns from muddy brown to completely clear (usually takes 5 minutes).
Once flushed, replace the end-caps and turn the water on. Use a high-lumen flashlight to inspect the spray pattern of every single nozzle across the 2-meter header. Any nozzle that spits or fans unevenly must be unscrewed and replaced immediately.
