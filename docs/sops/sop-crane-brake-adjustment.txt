Procedure for setting the air gap on the electro-hydraulic thruster brakes of the main ladle crane hoist.
Ensure the load block is resting on the ground and wire ropes are completely slack before touching the brake. Use feeler gauges to set the clearance between the brake shoe lining and the drum to exactly 0.5mm on both sides. If the gap is too large, the thruster will max out its stroke before applying full clamping force, allowing the load to slip. If too tight, the brake will drag when the motor runs, overheating the drum. Test the brake by lifting a test weight 1 meter and cutting the power.
