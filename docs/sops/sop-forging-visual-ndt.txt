Execution of the Standard Operating Procedure for Visual Inspection (SOP-821-013) on final forged components. After shot blasting, every critical forging must be visually inspected under 1000-lux lighting. Look for under-fill, scale pits, and trim tears along the flash line. Use a certified depth gauge on any visible surface anomaly. If a scratch exceeds 0.5mm in depth, route the piece to the rework yard per the Standard Operating Procedure for Workpiece Rejection and Rework (SOP-821-027). Mark the defect with a yellow paint marker and log the specific die number.
