Before a mould-width change, verify the new width, taper, segment settings, cooling recipe, dummy-bar position, and quality plan. Confirm both sides move symmetrically and the mechanical locks are engaged. Run the approved dry or low-risk check before steel enters. Record old and new width, stroke, taper, lock status, and operator verification. A recipe mismatch can create shell stress or surface defects. Second-person confirmation is required after a sequence interruption.  Record the equipment tag, readings with units, time, field verifier, and handover status. If the field condition differs from the screen, drawing, or job card, stop and resolve the difference before continuing. A clean closeout includes guards, barriers, drains, tools, temporary controls, and the exact condition for return to service. The handover should state the current operating mode, the last stable reading, the open risk, and the person or discipline responsible for the next check. If a temporary measure is used, give it an owner and review time. Do not leave a condition described only as “normal now”; explain what was observed, what evidence supports the decision, and what change would require stopping the equipment. This is how practical experience becomes usable plant knowledge without weakening the formal control system.
