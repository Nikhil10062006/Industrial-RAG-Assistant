Procedure for pressurizing the Pulverized Coal Injection (PCI) injection vessel prior to blowing coal into the Blast Furnace. This is a highly combustible environment; strict oxygen control is mandatory.
Before dropping pulverized coal from the upper lock hopper into the main injection vessel, verify the vessel's internal oxygen analyzer reads strictly below 4%. If O2 is above 4%, initiate an automated nitrogen purge. Once purged, open the equalizer valve and drop the coal. Close the dome valve. You must visually confirm on the HMI that the dome valve seal has inflated to 6 bar before introducing high-pressure conveying nitrogen.
Slowly open the main pressurization valve to bring the vessel up to 12 bar (must be at least 2 bar higher than the Blast Furnace hot blast pressure). Do not slam the pressurization valve open to 100%; the sudden shockwave will fluidize the coal dust too violently and blind the upper filter bags, causing a pressure relief blowout.
