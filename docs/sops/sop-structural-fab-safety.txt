Mandatory safety protocols for structural fabrication and erection works. A pre-start safety induction training by the Officer or Maintenance in-charge must be given to technicians and contractor workers before starting the job. All tools and tackles required for executing the job must be accumulated in the working zone area for checking. Keep stairways and walkways always free from any debris. Slippery or wet surfaces may cause a slip, trip, and fall while working. Always maintain 3 point contact while using any stairway approach to minimize the chances of injury due to a fall. Use of a Calibrated Gas Detector is mandatory for initial monitoring of Gas Levels at the gas work location. Work must proceed below 50 ppm only.
