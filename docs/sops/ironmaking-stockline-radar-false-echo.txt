Operating area: Blast furnace top. Use the approved procedure, identify the equipment and energy state, preserve the first indication, and keep people outside the hazard boundary. A stockline radar may show a stable or high reading when burden dust, condensation, or a charging stream creates a false echo. Compare radar quality, purge air, hopper sequence, burden weight, and alternate level indication from the approved position. Clean the window, correct purge, and prove the reading through several charge cycles before automatic charging is accepted. Record the equipment tag, material or heat identity, time, readings with units, people informed, and the final status. If a temporary control, bypass, manual setting, or alternate route is used, give it an owner and review time. Do not close the event because the alarm cleared or the equipment restarted once. Repeat the relevant test under the same load, speed, temperature, pressure, or material condition that caused the problem. At handover, state what was checked, what remains uncertain, and the exact condition that requires stopping the equipment. Practical plant knowledge is useful when it makes the next safe decision easier without replacing isolation, permit, quality, emergency, or engineering controls.
