Inspection and patching of the Sinter Plant Ignition Hood suspended refractory roof. The hood operates at 1150°C and uses a suspended anchor brick design. Thermal cycling causes these bricks to spall and occasionally drop onto the moving sinter bed.
During the weekly 8-hour maintenance stop, the cooling fan must run for 2 hours before the hood can be accessed. Access is via the side inspection doors. Do not stand directly under a suspect roof section. Use a long steel probing rod to firmly tap the bricks. A healthy brick rings with a high pitch; a cracked or detached brick yields a dull, hollow thud.
If a single brick has fallen, it must be patched immediately using high-alumina castable refractory rammed into the void. If more than three adjacent bricks are loose, the entire row's hanger castings must be replaced. Never start the main burners if the steel roof casing is exposed; the 1150°C flame will burn through the steel shell in 10 minutes.
