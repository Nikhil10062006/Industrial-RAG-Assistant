Operation of the Dry Fog System (DFS) for dust suppression in the Raw Material Handling System (RMHS). The fog operational running system should be operated and checked by a skilled operator. Ensure the air line valve is opened first, followed by the water line valve. Water pressure must be monitored closely between 1-1.5 kg/cm2, and air pressure strictly between 5-6 kg/cm2. Check the mixing of water and air to ensure true fog is forming. Before starting, the nozzle face must be cleaned first. The system should manually run before the equipment starts, and stop after the running equipment stops.
