A stave-flow test is performed only with the furnace controller and water-system operator aligned on the test window. Verify the circuit number, inlet and outlet valves, flow instrument, pressure gauges, temperature points, and alternate cooling path. Do not close a cooling circuit without confirming the approved duration and compensating controls. Compare indicated flow with the local sight glass or calibrated reference where available. Check for rising outlet temperature, vibration in the line, leakage at flexible connections, and a sudden change in neighboring circuits. If flow falls, do not immediately increase pump pressure; inspect the strainer, valve position, air lock, and return restriction. Cooling-water quality and recent refractory work should be noted because deposits can narrow a circuit slowly. Record circuit tag, inlet pressure, outlet pressure, flow, inlet and outlet temperature, test duration, and acceptance decision. A circuit that passes at low demand may be marginal during a hot operating period. The practical rule is to trend the difference between circuits, not only compare each one with a generic limit.
