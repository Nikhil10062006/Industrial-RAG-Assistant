Mechanical deslagging of hot metal Torpedo Cars prior to charging the Basic Oxygen Furnace (BOF). Blast furnace hot metal arrives with a layer of silica-rich slag that must be removed, or it will consume all our expensive lime fluxes in the BOF.
Tilt the torpedo car to roughly 45 degrees until the slag line is level with the lip. The operator in the deslagging pulpit will extend the mechanical rabble arm (a massive water-cooled steel rake). Gently lower the rabble head just beneath the crust of the slag—do not plunge it deep into the liquid iron. Pull the slag forward and scrape it off the lip into the slag pot below.
Watch the color of the stream. Slag is bright, crusty, and flows sluggishly. Liquid iron is blindingly bright white/yellow and sparks heavily. Stop scraping immediately when you see heavy sparking; you are dragging good iron into the waste pot and killing our yield. The rabble arm cooling water return temperature must stay below 60°C.
