Application and management of mold powder (flux) on the Continuous Caster mold during steady-state casting. Mold powder serves four critical functions: lubricating the copper mold, absorbing impurities, providing thermal insulation, and preventing steel oxidation.
The operator must maintain a dark, black layer of unreacted powder on the top surface at all times. If you see the red glow of liquid steel (a "bald spot"), you are starving the mold of lubrication, and a sticker breakout is imminent.
Add powder in small, frequent scoops using the aluminum shovel. Never dump an entire 10kg bag at once; this will cause "flux entrapment" where unmelted powder gets sucked deep into the solidifying steel shell, causing severe internal defects. Watch the consumption rate. A normal rate for medium carbon steel is 0.4 kg/ton. If consumption suddenly drops, the flux has bridged. Use a wire skimming rod to physically break the crust.
