Start-up and verification of the Continuous Caster mold oscillation table. The mold oscillator prevents the solidifying steel shell from sticking to the copper mold plates. Before engaging the dummy bar, power up the hydraulic power unit (HPU) for the oscillator and wait for the hydraulic temperature to reach 40°C. Cold oil will result in sluggish response and a jerky sine wave, leading to breakout risks.
Set the stroke length to 6mm for standard structural grades, and frequency should track automatically with casting speed (typically 120 cpm at 1.0 m/min). Run a dry test for two minutes. Monitor the waveform on the maintenance HMI. The negative strip time must remain strictly between 0.12 and 0.18 seconds. If the curve looks flattened at the top or bottom of the stroke, the leaf springs are likely fatigued or there is a mechanical bind in the eccentric shaft. Do not start casting if the wave is distorted. Switch to the standby oscillation cartridge and send the primary to the shop for rebuild.
