Before magnetic-particle inspection, confirm roll material, surface cleanliness, magnetizing direction, consumable condition, lighting, and equipment calibration. The inspector must know whether the method is suitable for the defect and material. Clean oil, scale, and loose paint without damaging the surface. Apply the field in the approved direction and inspect under the required light. A linear indication should be marked, photographed, and evaluated; do not wipe it away and assume it was contamination. Demagnetize and clean the roll after inspection as specified. Record roll number, area covered, direction, equipment check, indications, and disposition. New technicians often confuse a broad background pattern with a crack; compare with a reference block and ask the qualified inspector. Inspection results must travel with the roll card and be linked to the stand assignment.

Record the equipment tag, operating condition, readings with units, and person who accepted the result. Compare the actual field condition with the control-room indication and the approved drawing or job card. If any value is outside the normal band, hold the task and obtain the responsible specialist rather than making an undocumented adjustment. At handover, state what was checked, what was changed, what remains open, and the exact condition that would require a stop.
