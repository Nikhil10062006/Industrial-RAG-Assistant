Before changing a scale-breaker roll, isolate the line, release strip tension, secure the roll frame, and verify no strip can move from adjacent bridles. Inspect roll surface, bearings, chocks, guides, and adjustment screws. Confirm the replacement roll diameter and surface pattern match the product and process plan. After installation, set the gap evenly across the width using the approved gauge. Run an unloaded cycle and inspect for rubbing or uneven movement. Thread at low speed and watch strip centering, tension, and descaling effect. Record roll number, diameter, gap, pressure, strip grade, and first-coil observation. Too much reduction can create edge damage; too little leaves scale and increases acid demand. Do not copy the previous gap if the roll diameter or strip grade changed. The roll card should show the actual setting used, not just the target from the recipe.

Record the equipment tag, operating condition, readings with units, and person who accepted the result. Compare the actual field condition with the control-room indication and the approved drawing or job card. If any value is outside the normal band, hold the task and obtain the responsible specialist rather than making an undocumented adjustment. At handover, state what was checked, what was changed, what remains open, and the exact condition that would require a stop.
