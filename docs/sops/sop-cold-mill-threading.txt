Coil threading procedure for the Tandem Cold Mill (TCM). The TCM reduces pickled steel strip from 3.0mm down to 0.5mm at extremely high speeds.
Load the master coil onto the payoff reel and engage the hold-down snubber roll. Peel the head of the coil and jog it forward into Stand 1. The strip must be perfectly centered on the entry guides. If the strip enters Stand 1 off-center by more than 15mm, it will "walk" completely off the rolls by the time it reaches Stand 5, causing a catastrophic high-speed cobble.
Apply 10% tension to the payoff reel to keep the strip taut. Once the strip head passes through Stand 1, apply the rolling emulsion spray immediately to prevent roll scratching. Jog the head through the remaining stands, attaching it to the tension reel mandrel. Ensure exactly two full wraps are securely on the mandrel before transferring mill control to "Auto-Run" and ramping up to run speed.
