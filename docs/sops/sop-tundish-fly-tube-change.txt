Procedure for executing a "flying" Submerged Entry Nozzle (SEN) tube change on the Continuous Caster. This allows us to replace a worn refractory tube without stopping the casting sequence. This is a high-speed, synchronized maneuver requiring two operators on the mold deck.
The pulpit operator will reduce casting speed to 0.4 m/min and throttle the slide gate to a minimum trickle. The primary operator must wear full aluminized gear and a face shield. Upon the "GO" signal, Operator 1 releases the pneumatic locking pins on the tube carriage. Operator 2 rapidly slides the pre-heated, red-hot replacement SEN into the guide rails, which simultaneously pushes the old, worn SEN out the other side.
This entire physical motion must be completed in under 4 seconds. If you hesitate mid-push, the liquid steel will freeze between the two nozzles, permanently jamming the carriage and forcing an emergency abort of the entire strand. Once the new tube is seated, immediately engage the locking pins, inject argon to prevent air aspiration, and signal the pulpit to ramp back up to casting speed.
