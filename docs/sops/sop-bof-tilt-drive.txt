Procedure for the pre-blow operational check of the Basic Oxygen Furnace (BOF) primary tilt drive mechanism. This must be conducted at the start of every shift before the first scrap charge. Check the gearbox oil level on the primary bull gear; it should be within the upper quadrant of the sight glass. The lube oil circulating pump must show a discharge pressure between 3.5 and 4.2 bar.
Test the primary mechanical brakes by attempting a micro-tilt (2 degrees) with the brakes artificially engaged via the test panel. The vessel must not drift. If any slippage occurs, the shift mechanical fitter must tighten the brake bands before the vessel is cleared for hot metal. Inspect the trunnion ring for any new thermal cracks, particularly around the drive-side pin. Log the bull gear temperature via the IR sensor readout; normal operating baseline is 65°C. Anything above 85°C indicates bearing wear or lube failure and requires immediate mechanical escalation before the next heat.
