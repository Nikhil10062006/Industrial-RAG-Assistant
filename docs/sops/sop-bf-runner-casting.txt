Procedure for casting a new main iron runner on the Blast Furnace cast house floor. The refractory trough handles 1500°C molten iron and highly corrosive slag. After tearing out the old refractory, ensure the steel runner box is completely dry and free of debris. Position the steel former (the mold) perfectly in the center; if it is off by even 10mm, the wear profile will be asymmetric, and the iron will burn through the steel box within a week.
Mix the low-cement, high-alumina castable with strictly 4.5% water by weight. Too much water makes it easy to pour but destroys its high-temperature strength. Pour the castable while simultaneously running the pneumatic poker vibrators to remove air bubbles. You must vibrate the material until a slight sheen of water appears on the surface, then stop immediately to prevent aggregate segregation. Allow it to cure for 24 hours before initiating the gas bake-out curve.
