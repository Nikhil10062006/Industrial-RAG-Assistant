Test pot-heater gas valves only under the burner-management and gas-safety procedure. Confirm pot temperature, line status, isolation, gas detection, ventilation, and ignition-source controls. Check valve position feedback against the field indicator and perform the approved partial-stroke or leak test. A valve that closes on the screen but leaks physically is a serious defect. Do not bypass flame supervision or repeatedly attempt ignition after a failure without purge. Record gas pressure, valve travel time, flame signal, exhaust condition, and test result. Inspect burners for dross, blocked tips, and unstable flame. Hot-dip equipment can remain hot after the heater is off, so keep heat PPE and barriers in place. If a valve sticks, repair it under isolation; do not increase actuator pressure to force movement. The acceptance record must show who verified both gas tightness and burner sequence.

Record the equipment tag, operating condition, readings with units, and person who accepted the result. Compare the actual field condition with the control-room indication and the approved drawing or job card. If any value is outside the normal band, hold the task and obtain the responsible specialist rather than making an undocumented adjustment. At handover, state what was checked, what was changed, what remains open, and the exact condition that would require a stop.
