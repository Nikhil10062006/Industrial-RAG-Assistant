Procedure for lifting the inner protective cover (bell) off a Batch Annealing Furnace base after the cooling cycle. The inner bell contains a protective Nitrogen/Hydrogen atmosphere to prevent the steel coils from oxidizing.
Do not authorize the crane to lift the bell until the base thermocouple reads below 120°C. Lifting the bell hotter than this will expose the steel coils to atmospheric oxygen, causing them to flash-oxidize and turn completely blue, ruining the surface finish.
Verify the base purge sequence is complete and the atmosphere under the bell is 100% Nitrogen. The crane operator must center the hook perfectly over the bell eyelet to prevent dragging the bell against the stacked coils. Lift the bell slowly for the first meter. If the bell sticks to the sand seal, do not force the hoist; lower it, physically break the crusted sand seal with a pry bar, and re-attempt the lift.
