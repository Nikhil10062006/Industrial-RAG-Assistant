Before changing a grinder wheel, verify machine isolation, wheel specification, ring test or supplier acceptance, guard condition, flange cleanliness, and spindle compatibility. Inspect the new wheel for cracks or moisture damage and store it correctly before use. Never use a wheel that has been dropped or has an unknown history. Mount the wheel with the approved blotters and torque sequence. Stand clear during the guarded test run and allow the specified idle time before presenting the roll. Check coolant flow, spark guards, dressing tool, and emergency stop. Record wheel number, grade, speed rating, spindle, dressing result, and operator. A wheel that fits the spindle can still be wrong for the material or speed. Do not increase feed to recover time after a delayed change. Grinding defects may be transferred into the roll and then into many coils.

Record the equipment tag, operating condition, readings with units, and person who accepted the result. Compare the actual field condition with the control-room indication and the approved drawing or job card. If any value is outside the normal band, hold the task and obtain the responsible specialist rather than making an undocumented adjustment. At handover, state what was checked, what was changed, what remains open, and the exact condition that would require a stop.
