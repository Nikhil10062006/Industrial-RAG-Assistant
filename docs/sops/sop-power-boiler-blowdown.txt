Continuous and Intermittent blowdown procedures for the 100 TPH Captive Power Plant Steam Boilers. As the boiler evaporates water into steam, dissolved solids (particularly silica) concentrate in the boiler drum. If silica exceeds 0.5 ppm, it will carry over into the steam and plate out like glass on the turbine blades, destroying them.
The Continuous Blowdown (CBD) valve on the upper drum should be modulated automatically by the conductivity analyzer, typically kept around 5% open. However, sludge and heavy phosphates will still settle in the bottom water wall headers.
To clear this sludge, perform a manual Intermittent Blowdown (IBD) every 8 hours. Open the quick-acting IBD valve completely for exactly 10 seconds, then slam it shut. Never leave an IBD valve open for more than 15 seconds, or you will drain the boiler drum, tripping the boiler on Low Water Level and potentially overheating the water wall tubes. Always log the exact time of the blowdown so the chemistry lab can correlate their samples.
