Startup protocol for the Sinter Plant Main Induced Draft (ID) Exhaust Fan. This is a massive 5MW critical asset; improper startup will cause severe grid voltage dips or catastrophic vibration trips. Ensure the sinter strand is stopped and the main exhaust damper is pinned at a maximum of 10% open. Starting the fan with a wide-open damper will overload the motor and trip the 11kV breaker.
Start the jacking oil pumps and verify lift pressure is >120 bar on both bearings. Start the lube oil cooler fans. Initiate the main motor start sequence. The rotor will take roughly 45 seconds to reach the synchronous speed of 990 RPM. Watch the vibration monitors intently during the critical speed passage (around 600 RPM)—vibration will spike up to 7mm/s but must settle below 2.5mm/s at full speed. Once stabilized, gradually open the main damper to 40% over a span of 5 minutes. If bearing temperatures exceed 75°C during this phase, trip the fan immediately and check the lube oil heat exchangers for cooling water flow.
