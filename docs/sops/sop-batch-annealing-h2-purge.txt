Atmosphere purging sequence for the Cold Rolling Mill Batch Annealing Furnaces (BAF). BAFs use a 100% pure Hydrogen atmosphere to prevent steel oxidation during the 72-hour heating cycle. Mixing Hydrogen with atmospheric oxygen at high temperatures is highly explosive.
Before opening the Hydrogen inlet valve, the inner furnace bell must be rigorously purged with pure Nitrogen. Flow Nitrogen at 50 Nm3/hr for a minimum of 45 minutes. You must physically take a sample from the base vent using the portable oxygen analyzer. The O2 level must strictly read below 1.0%.
Simultaneously, check the dew point of the Nitrogen exhaust. It must be below -40°C. If the dew point is high, there is water trapped under the base (usually from a leaking cooling coil), which will turn to steam and rust the coils. Only when O2 < 1.0% and dew point < -40°C is the supervisor authorized to turn the physical key switch that unlocks the Hydrogen mass flow controllers.
