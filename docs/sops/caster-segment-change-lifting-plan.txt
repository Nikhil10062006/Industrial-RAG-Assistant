Before lifting a caster segment, confirm weight, center of gravity, approved lifting lugs, sling capacity, crane route, landing stand, and exclusion zone. Inspect lugs, pins, shackles, slings, hooks, and tag lines. Remove water and loose scale that could fall during lifting. The segment must be drained and isolated from hydraulic, electrical, and cooling connections. Take the load just clear of its supports and hold to check balance. If it tilts, lower it and correct the rigging; do not guide it by hand near pinch points. Travel slowly with a designated signalman and no one beneath the suspended load. Place the segment on the marked stand and secure it before releasing the crane. Record segment number, lifting gear, inspection, weight confirmation, and any damaged connection. A segment that was lifted safely last time may have a changed center of gravity after refractory or roll work.

Record the equipment tag, operating condition, readings with units, and person who accepted the result. Compare the actual field condition with the control-room indication and the approved drawing or job card. If any value is outside the normal band, hold the task and obtain the responsible specialist rather than making an undocumented adjustment. At handover, state what was checked, what was changed, what remains open, and the exact condition that would require a stop.
