Calibration check for the Raw Material Proportioning Weigh-Feeders in the Sinter Plant. The mix of iron ore fines, limestone, and coke breeze must be heavily controlled. A 0.5% error in coke breeze addition will cause the sinter bed to either freeze (too little) or melt into a solid glass-like block (too much).
Every Sunday day shift, perform a dynamic calibration test on the coke breeze weigh-feeder. Stop the main collector belt. Run the coke feeder at its 10 t/hr setpoint for exactly 6 minutes into the divert chute and calibration skip. Weigh the skip on the certified static scale. The weight must be exactly 1000 kg +/- 5 kg. If the deviation is greater than 1%, adjust the load cell span on the Siemens PLC. Ensure the belt scraper is adjusted tight to the head pulley; carry-back on the return belt will falsify the load cell readings.
