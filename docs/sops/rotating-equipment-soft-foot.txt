Before aligning a motor, pump, fan, or gearbox, check for soft foot with the coupling disconnected or in the approved condition. Isolate the equipment, clean the base, loosen one foot at a time, and measure movement with the approved instrument. Do not place fingers under a foot or remove all bolts at once. Correct shimming only after confirming base condition, hold-down bolt condition, and pipe strain. Recheck alignment after tightening. Record each foot reading, shim thickness, bolt torque, and final alignment. A machine can show acceptable coupling readings while a soft foot causes movement, vibration, and repeat seal failure when energized. Veteran fitters check the base and connected piping before chasing the final thousandth. Restore guards and run the machine under load, comparing vibration and temperature with the pre-work record.

Record the equipment tag, operating condition, readings with units, and person who accepted the result. Compare the actual field condition with the control-room indication and the approved drawing or job card. If any value is outside the normal band, hold the task and obtain the responsible specialist rather than making an undocumented adjustment. At handover, state what was checked, what was changed, what remains open, and the exact condition that would require a stop.
