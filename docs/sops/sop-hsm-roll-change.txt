This outlines the automated and manual steps for a work roll change on Hot Strip Mill Finishing Stand F4. Initiate the sequence only when the mill speed is at absolute zero and the main drive lock-out is confirmed on the pulpit HMI. First, shut off the roll cooling headers and gap the rolls to 150mm. Engage the roll extractor carriage. The hydraulic locking cylinders must show a pressure drop below 10 bar before extraction begins.
If the extractor jams mid-pull, do not override the hydraulic pressure limit (set at 120 bar). Often, scale buildup on the chocks causes this. Manually hose down the chock guides with high-pressure water, clear the scale, and re-attempt. Once the worn rolls are out, verify the backup roll surfaces for any visible spalling before inserting the new set. Drive the new rolls in, engage the locks, and visually verify the locking pins are fully seated through the chock holes. Zero the mill gap at a load of 1000 tons before returning control to the pulpit.
