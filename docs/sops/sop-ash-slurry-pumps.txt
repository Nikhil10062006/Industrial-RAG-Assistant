Operational swapping of the Captive Power Plant bottom ash slurry pumps. These pumps handle a highly abrasive mix of water and boiler ash, destroying their impellers every few months.
Before shutting down the running pump (Pump A), you must start the standby pump (Pump B). Open the gland sealing water supply to Pump B and verify pressure is 2 bar higher than the pump casing pressure to keep ash out of the stuffing box.
Start Pump B against a closed discharge valve. Slowly open Pump B's discharge valve while simultaneously throttling closed Pump A's discharge valve to maintain steady flow in the pipeline. Do not let the pipeline flow drop below 2.5 m/s, or the heavy ash will settle out and plug the 5-kilometer pipeline to the ash pond. Once Pump A is isolated, run clean water through it for 10 minutes to flush the casing before shutting it down completely.
