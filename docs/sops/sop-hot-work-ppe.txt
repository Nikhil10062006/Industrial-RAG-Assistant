PPE compliance for hot work (welding/gas cutting) near rotating equipment. The executing technician must use an FR jacket and leather gloves. Do not touch anything with bare hands and keep safe distances from hot surfaces. When working at height, take all necessary precautions as per the permit checklist, use a standard Safety Belt, and lock the hook at the proper place. Do not remove the full body harness while elevated. Do not walk over open drain areas or running equipment. Keep a safe distance from rotating equipment and do not walk while speaking on a mobile phone. All employees performing these tasks should be full stomach.
