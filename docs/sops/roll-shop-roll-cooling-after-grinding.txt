After grinding a roll, cool it in the approved location and rate before final measurement. Do not quench a hot roll unless the material and procedure explicitly permit it. Rapid uneven cooling can change geometry and create residual stress. Measure roll body temperature at several points and compare operator and drive sides. Verify diameter, crown, surface roughness, runout, and journal condition only after the roll is within the specified measurement band. Record grinding removal, wheel, coolant condition, temperature, final dimensions, and inspector. A roll that measures correctly while hot may fall outside tolerance after cooling. Keep the roll supported at the designated locations to prevent sag and protect the surface. If grinding burn, chatter marks, or discoloration are found, hold the roll for technical review. Do not polish over a defect without documenting the underlying cause.

Record the equipment tag, operating condition, readings with units, and person who accepted the result. Compare the actual field condition with the control-room indication and the approved drawing or job card. If any value is outside the normal band, hold the task and obtain the responsible specialist rather than making an undocumented adjustment. At handover, state what was checked, what was changed, what remains open, and the exact condition that would require a stop.
