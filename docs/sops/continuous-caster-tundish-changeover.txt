Plan a tundish changeover with the caster controller, crane operator, and steelmaking shift team. Confirm the spare tundish is dry, preheated, identified for the heat sequence, and fitted with the correct nozzle and slide-gate assembly. Check the tundish car path and confirm no tools or personnel are inside the travel zone.

Reduce speed or follow the approved flying-change sequence. Watch mould level, stopper response, ladle stream, and tundish weight continuously. The operator must not rely on the planned timing if the actual tundish level falls differently.

When the new tundish is positioned, confirm nozzle alignment before opening. Keep the receiving area clear of splashes and do not stand beneath the tundish. If the nozzle does not align, stop the movement and use the approved correction method. Do not push the tundish by hand.

After the change, verify mould level stability, argon flow, casting speed, secondary cooling, and the first slab head condition. Record the old and new tundish numbers, change time, temperature, nozzle behavior, and any manual intervention. A successful changeover is one where the next heat starts with known conditions, not merely one where the strand did not stop.
