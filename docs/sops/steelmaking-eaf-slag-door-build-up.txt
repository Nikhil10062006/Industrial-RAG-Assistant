Before clearing slag-door buildup, confirm the furnace is in the approved condition, the door mechanism is isolated, and the surrounding platform and drop zone are clear. Slag can remain hot beneath a crust and may fall without warning. Use the designated tool and shield from the approved position; never stand below the door or strike the shell directly. Check water lines, refractory, door hinges, and nearby cables for heat damage. If the buildup is blocking slag flow, coordinate with the furnace controller before any adjustment to tilt or power. A sudden release can send slag toward the tap floor. Keep wet tools and hoses away from hot slag. Record furnace stage, buildup location, method used, door condition, and any damaged refractory. New operators often think the door is safe when the visible slag is black; the interior can still be glowing and liquid. Clear only what the procedure allows and escalate a recurring buildup for process or refractory review.
