This standard operating procedure covers the preparation and execution of opening the Blast Furnace taphole. Prior to drilling, ensure the mud gun is fully loaded with at least 1.5 tons of taphole clay and is positioned accurately at the home standby location. Verify the drill bit is sharp and the rod is straight; a bent rod will cause an off-angle hole and severe splash.
Begin drilling at a 10-12 degree downward angle. The drill speed should be maintained at 400 RPM with steady feed pressure. If you hit a hard spot around 1.2 meters in, do not force the hydraulic feed. Retract slightly and pulse the feed. Once the drill breaks through the skull (usually at 2.2 to 2.5 meters depending on hearth wear), retract the drill immediately to prevent the rod from melting in the iron stream. If the drill fails to break through, swap to an oxygen lance. Ensure full aluminized PPE is worn during lancing. Never stand directly in front of the taphole; always position yourself behind the primary splash shield.
