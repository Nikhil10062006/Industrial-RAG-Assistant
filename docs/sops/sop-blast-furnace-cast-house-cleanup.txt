Post-cast cleanup protocol for the Blast Furnace cast house floor. Liquid iron and slag spills must be managed immediately before they fuse to the concrete deck.
Deploy the Bobcat loader with the pneumatic breaker attachment while the spilled slag is still semi-solid (dull red). Push debris into the designated slag pits. Ensure areas around the mudgun and drill carriage tracks are completely swept. Any debris left on the tracks will derail the heavy hydraulic machinery during the next tap. Dump a fresh layer of dry silica sand over the deck to prevent the next spill from sticking.
