Execution of a main oxygen lance change on the Basic Oxygen Furnace. Lances must be swapped when the copper tip shows severe pitting or the cooling water delta-T exceeds 15°C.
Bring the vessel to the vertical position and lock it. Hoist the old lance to the top maintenance deck. Shut off and mechanically lock out the main oxygen supply and the primary cooling water supply and return lines. Crack the bleed valves to verify zero pressure before unbolting the massive 8-inch flange connections.
When rigging the new lance, ensure the crane sling is positioned precisely at the designated center-of-gravity lift point. A 20-meter lance will swing violently if rigged off-center. Torque the oxygen flange bolts in a star pattern to 300 Nm. A leaking oxygen flange at the top of the lance will ignite instantly when the blow starts. Pressure test the water jacket at 10 bar before clearing the lance for operation.
