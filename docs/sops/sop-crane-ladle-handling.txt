Safety and operational SOP for handling full molten metal ladles (up to 300 tons) using the Melt Shop Teeming Crane. The operator must visually verify the primary hoist hook trunnions are fully seated in the ladle bails before lifting. The safety latch must drop cleanly into place. Lift the ladle exactly 6 inches off the transfer car and stop.
Hold the load for 10 seconds to perform a static brake test. The load must not creep downwards. If any downward creep is observed, lower the ladle back to the car immediately and call electrical maintenance to inspect the hoist shoe brakes. During bridge travel, keep the ladle as close to the floor as practically possible—no higher than 1.5 meters above obstacles. Never travel over the heads of personnel. When approaching the continuous caster turret, reduce bridge speed to 10% to prevent pendulum swinging of the ladle. Smooth, incremental joystick inputs are required; aggressive plugging or reversing of the crane motors will cause dangerous load sway.
