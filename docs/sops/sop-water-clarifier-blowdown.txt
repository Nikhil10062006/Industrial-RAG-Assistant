Sludge blowdown procedure for the Plant Primary Water Clarifier. The clarifier settles out mud and suspended solids from the raw river water using coagulant chemicals.
The heavy sludge settles at the bottom cone of the tank and must be blown down every 4 hours. If the sludge blanket rises too high, it will carry over into the clean water launder, sending mud into the plant's heat exchangers.
Open the pneumatic blowdown valve on the bottom of the clarifier. You must physically stand at the sludge discharge trench and watch the effluent. It will initially come out thick, black, and viscous. Leave the valve open until the discharge transitions to a lighter, watery brown color (usually 3 to 5 minutes). Do not blow it down until it runs completely clear, or you will lose the active sludge blanket needed to trap incoming dirt. Close the valve and log the blowdown duration.
