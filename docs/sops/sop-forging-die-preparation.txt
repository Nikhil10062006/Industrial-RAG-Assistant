Maintenance rules under the Standard Operating Procedure for Die Preparation and Maintenance (SOP-821-003). The forging dies handle extreme localized pressures. Before mounting the upper and lower dies in the 5000-ton press, preheat them to 250°C using localized gas burners. Striking a 1200°C billet with a cold die will cause the die steel to shatter instantly from thermal shock. Furthermore, follow the Standard Operating Procedure for Lubrication and Die Release (SOP-821-021) meticulously. Spray the graphite-water lubricant evenly across the impression cavity between every single strike. If you under-lube, the forging will stick; if you over-lube, trapped steam causes under-fill defects.
