Before swapping a stove dome-temperature sensor, confirm which stove is on blast, the alternate sensor status, isolation points, and safe access temperature. The replacement must have the correct range, thermowell, wiring, and calibration record. Do not remove a sensor from a pressurized or hot location without the approved permit and cooling period. Compare the suspect sensor with adjacent dome thermocouples, flue temperature, gas flow, and stove status before deciding it is faulty. When installing the replacement, protect the lead from heat and vibration, maintain insertion depth, and verify polarity. Perform a loop check from sensor to control screen, then compare with a reference reading during a stable stove condition. Record as-found value, reference value, sensor serial number, insertion depth, loop result, and time returned to service. A sensor that reads smoothly but is mounted too shallow can produce a believable, biased temperature. Do not retune stove changeover logic to compensate for a bad installation.
