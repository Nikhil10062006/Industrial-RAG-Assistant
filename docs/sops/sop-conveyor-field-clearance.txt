Field clearance protocol prior to starting high-speed conveyor belts. The shift in-charge must ensure no person is working on the conveyor belts. Verify that all belts are in the center position. Check the condition of the idlers, deck plates, and side skirts, looking specifically for material jamming on the deck plate and verifying safety devices before giving clearance to the control room. Ensure all safety devices in all conveyor belts are working in a healthy condition and are not bypassed from the PLC. Check whether the hooter/prewarn is blowing or not before starting the circuit.
