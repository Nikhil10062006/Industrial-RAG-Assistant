A breakout drill is conducted with the strand in a safe training or approved operating state and with all participants aware that no real breakout is present. Review the alarm path, emergency stop, mould-level response, withdrawal stop, water isolation, evacuation route, communication channel, and muster point. Do not create a simulated condition by defeating a safety device on a live strand. The coordinator should time the response and record where confusion occurs. Operators must identify the first action, the safe direction of movement, and who controls the equipment. Maintenance and electrical responders should not enter the strand area until the mock isolation is confirmed. After the drill, compare actual response with the approved procedure and correct unclear labels, inaccessible switches, or conflicting commands. Veteran note: drills fail most often at handover between pulpit, platform, crane, and maintenance teams. A perfect alarm response in the control room is not enough if the field team does not know the safe boundary.
