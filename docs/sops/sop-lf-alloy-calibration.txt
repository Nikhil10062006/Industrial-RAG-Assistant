Routine calibration of the Ladle Furnace (LF) bulk alloy weigh-hoppers. Accurate alloy addition is the only way to hit tight steel chemistry grades. A 10kg error on a ferro-vanadium addition will scrap the entire 300-ton heat.
This must be done while the hoppers are completely empty. Ensure the pneumatic discharge gates are locked closed. Navigate to the load cell calibration screen on the maintenance PLC.
Hang the certified 500kg test weights symmetrically on the four corners of the weigh-hopper suspension frame. The HMI must read 2000kg +/- 2kg. If it drifts beyond this, the load cells are likely bound by dust or mechanical interference. Physically inspect the rubber isolation boots around the hopper; they must be completely flexible. If they are packed hard with alloy dust, the hopper cannot hang freely, causing the load cells to under-report the weight. Clean the boots and re-weigh.
