A ladle-temperature camera image became distorted during a heat. Inspection from the safe position found a cracked protective window caused by thermal shock after a cold cleaning spray. The camera itself remained functional but its view was unreliable. The ladle was managed using the approved alternate measurement while the window was replaced. Record camera, window condition, cleaning method, temperature, and alternate measurement. Veteran note: a camera can fail optically without a signal alarm. Do not wash a hot optical window unless the equipment procedure allows it. Inspect image quality, not only camera status.

The condition was added to the shift handover because it may recur under similar weather, load, material, or maintenance conditions. The immediate response protected people first, then preserved evidence for the investigation. Do not treat an unusual event as harmless because the plant recovered. Check adjacent equipment, temporary repairs, alarms, and records from the affected window. The practical lesson is to identify the early sign, the safe boundary, the escalation point, and the evidence required before normal operation is restored.
