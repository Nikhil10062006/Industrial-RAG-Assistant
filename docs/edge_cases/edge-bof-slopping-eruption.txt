Scenario: Violent, uncontrollable slag eruption (slopping) over the mouth of the Basic Oxygen Furnace during the middle of the oxygen blow.
Action Taken: The operator had added a large batch of iron ore pellets late into the blow to cool down a hot heat. The sudden addition of oxygen-rich ore into a highly carbonized bath triggered a massive, instantaneous carbon boil. The slag foamed violently, expanding to five times its normal volume, and cascaded over the lip of the vessel, raining hundreds of tons of liquid fire down into the doghouse and pit below.
The operator immediately initiated a "Dynamic Lance Lift" and cut the oxygen flow to zero. The floor was evacuated. The liquid slag buried the transfer car tracks and severed the limit switch cabling.
Lesson Learned: Never use bulk iron ore for cooling if carbon levels are still high. The thermodynamic reaction is too aggressive. Use steel scrap for late-stage cooling instead, as it melts slowly without triggering an oxygen-carbon explosion.
