Scenario: Severe summer dust storm choking the intakes of the main plant air compressors.
During a massive dust storm in May, the ambient particulate load overwhelmed the primary intake filters on Compressor Station 1 within minutes. The differential pressure (delta-P) alarms spiked to 500 mmWC. The auto-pulse cleaning system ran continuously but couldn't keep up, starving the compressors of air and causing them to approach the surge line.
Action Taken: Do not rely on the auto-pulse in this scenario. We manually bypassed the primary filter bank and swapped air intake to the secondary internal roof-space louvers, which draw air from inside the relatively cleaner compressor building. We also slightly unloaded the compressors to 80% capacity to move them away from the surge margin.
Lesson Learned: In extreme dust events, auto-filter cleaning will fail. Know the location of the manual intake swap dampers and reduce machine load immediately to prevent an aerodynamic stall.
