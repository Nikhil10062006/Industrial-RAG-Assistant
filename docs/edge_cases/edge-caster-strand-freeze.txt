Scenario: Sudden loss of all hydraulic pressure to the Continuous Caster withdrawal straightener drives while casting at 1.2 m/min.
Action Taken: The strand physically stopped moving. The steel inside the water-cooled copper mold was still freezing. We had exactly 45 seconds before the solidifying steel shell would weld itself completely to the copper mold plates, destroying a $250,000 mold and causing a multi-day outage.
The pulpit operator immediately slammed the tundish slide gate closed to stop the flow of liquid steel. The mechanical team ran to the local hydraulic valve stand and manually depressed the directional spool valves using screwdrivers (overriding the dead PLC) to force the drive rolls to reverse. We managed to pull the dummy bar and the hot slab backward out of the mold just seconds before it froze solid.
Lesson Learned: You must know how to manually actuate your local hydraulic valves. When the automation dies and liquid steel is freezing, a screwdriver on a solenoid pin is the only thing that will save the machine.
