Scenario: Brake failure on a diesel locomotive pulling three full 300-ton torpedo cars down the 1% grade toward the Melt Shop.
Action Taken: The driver lost pneumatic brake pressure due to a burst main air line between the cars. Nearly 1500 tons of dead weight were accelerating out of control toward the congested BOF charging bay. The driver immediately radioed "Code Red Runaway." The yardmaster sprinted to the manual track switch box at junction 4 and threw the points, diverting the runaway train onto the emergency sand-track siding. The locomotive plowed into the deep sand and derailed safely, keeping the torpedo cars upright.
Lesson Learned: Never bypass the "dead man's switch" in the locomotive cab, and always ensure the emergency sand-tracks are raked and free of debris. If that track had been blocked, the loco would have breached the BOF structural columns.
