A furnace pressure transmitter showed stable neutral pressure while flames rolled out of a door. The impulse tap was plugged with scale and could not sense the actual positive pressure. The furnace was moved to the safe state, the tap isolated and cleared, and pressure compared with a temporary reference. Record transmitter value, local flame behavior, tap condition, furnace draft, and repair. Lesson: a quiet pressure trend can be a blocked impulse line. Check taps, tracing, drains, and reference legs when process behavior disagrees with instrumentation. Do not retune dampers around a dead signal.

The condition was added to the shift handover because it may recur under similar weather, load, material, or maintenance conditions. The immediate response protected people first, then preserved evidence for the investigation. Do not treat an unusual event as harmless because the plant recovered. Check adjacent equipment, temporary repairs, alarms, and records from the affected window. The practical lesson is to identify the early sign, the safe boundary, the escalation point, and the evidence required before normal operation is restored.
