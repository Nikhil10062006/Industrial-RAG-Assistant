Scenario: The ladle slide gate froze solid mid-cast. The hydraulic cylinder was pushing with 150 bar of force, but the refractory plates refused to slide closed, leaving a full stream of steel pouring into the tundish.
Action Taken: The continuous caster operator saw the tundish level rising uncontrollably toward an overflow. He immediately hit the emergency E-stop to halt the strand and deployed the emergency launder chute. We manually swung the ladle over the emergency overflow pit and let the remaining 50 tons of steel drain safely onto the ground.
Lesson Learned: The slide gate froze because the argon cooling line to the gate mechanism had pinched, allowing the mechanical springs holding the plates together to overheat and warp, locking the plates. Always verify argon flow to the slide gate mechanism before opening a ladle.
