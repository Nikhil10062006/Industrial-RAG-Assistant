A rail wagon brake hose did not pressurize during winter preparation. The hose was frozen at a low sag point, while the locomotive supply pressure was normal. The wagon was not moved. The hose was isolated and thawed using the approved method, then inspected for cracking and moisture. Drainage and hose routing were corrected. Record temperature, hose position, pressure, thaw method, and brake test. Lesson: a normal supply gauge does not prove every wagon circuit is available. Never use a torch near a brake hose or fuel system. A complete brake test is required before movement.

The condition was added to the shift handover because it may recur under similar weather, load, material, or maintenance conditions. The immediate response protected people first, then preserved evidence for the investigation. Do not treat an unusual event as harmless because the plant recovered. Check adjacent equipment, temporary repairs, alarms, and records from the affected window. The practical lesson is to identify the early sign, the safe boundary, the escalation point, and the evidence required before normal operation is restored.
