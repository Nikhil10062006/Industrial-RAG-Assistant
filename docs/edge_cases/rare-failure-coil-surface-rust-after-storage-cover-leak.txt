A coil developed localized rust under a storage cover after a monsoon storm. The coil was dry when covered, but water pooled on the cover and wicked through a seam onto the top wrap. The support saddle was sound and the rust pattern matched the cover edge. The coil was placed on quality hold, the cover replaced, and drainage improved. Record coil, cover, weather, storage duration, rust location, and packaging condition. Lesson: a cover that keeps rain off can also trap water against steel. Inspect covers, slopes, seams, ventilation, and floor drainage. Do not ship or uncoil affected material before quality disposition. The storage standard should define when covers are required and how they are supported.

The condition was added to the shift handover because it may recur under similar weather, load, material, or maintenance conditions. The immediate response protected people first, then preserved evidence for the investigation. Do not treat an unusual event as harmless because the plant recovered. Check adjacent equipment, temporary repairs, alarms, and records from the affected window. The practical lesson is to identify the early sign, the safe boundary, the escalation point, and the evidence required before normal operation is restored.
