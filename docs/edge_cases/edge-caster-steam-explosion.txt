Scenario: A massive sticker breakout on the Continuous Caster where liquid steel tore through the copper mold and directly hit the high-pressure cooling water jacket.
Action Taken: The 1500°C steel instantly vaporized the 10-bar cooling water. The resulting volumetric steam explosion blew the massive 15-ton mold cover straight up into the air, snapping the heavy hold-down bolts like twigs. The blast shattered the tundish car rails and destroyed the oscillator table. Fortunately, the operator was in the pulpit, not on the deck.
Lesson Learned: We retrofitted the primary cooling water return headers with explosive-actuated rupture discs. Now, if a breakout breaches the water jacket and pressure spikes instantly, the discs blow and vent the steam safely through large exhaust pipes to the outside of the building, saving the mechanical structure of the machine from catastrophic destruction.
