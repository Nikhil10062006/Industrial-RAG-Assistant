Scenario: Total loss of pneumatic brake pressure on a diesel locomotive pulling three full 300-ton torpedo cars down the 1% grade toward the Melt Shop.
Action Taken: The train, weighing over 1500 tons, was accelerating out of control toward the congested BOF charging aisle. The driver radioed a "Code Red Runaway." The yardmaster sprinted to the manual track switch box at junction 4 and threw the heavy mechanical points, diverting the runaway train onto the emergency sand-track siding. The locomotive plowed into the deep sand bed, safely decelerating and derailing in a controlled manner, keeping the torpedo cars upright and intact.
Lesson Learned: The emergency sand tracks are not parking lots. They must be raked weekly and kept completely free of debris. If that track had been blocked by an empty wagon, the runaway train would have breached the BOF structural columns.
