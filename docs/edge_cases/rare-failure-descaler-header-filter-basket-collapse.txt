Descaler header pressure became unstable after a filter basket collapsed internally. The housing differential-pressure indication was low because the basket no longer supported the filter media. Fragments moved downstream and blocked several nozzles. The header was isolated, the filter basket replaced, and all nozzles inspected before restart. Record filter condition, pressure, flow, fragments, and downstream impact. Lesson: low differential pressure can mean a failed filter, not a clean one. Inspect basket integrity during maintenance and compare nozzle plugging with filter history.

The condition was added to the shift handover because it may recur under similar weather, load, material, or maintenance conditions. The immediate response protected people first, then preserved evidence for the investigation. Do not treat an unusual event as harmless because the plant recovered. Check adjacent equipment, temporary repairs, alarms, and records from the affected window. The practical lesson is to identify the early sign, the safe boundary, the escalation point, and the evidence required before normal operation is restored.
