Scenario: Uncontrollable algae and biological bloom in the Sinter Plant main cooling tower basin during the peak summer monsoon season.
The humidity and heat caused a massive biological bloom that the standard chlorine dosing couldn't handle. The algae formed thick biofilms inside the heat exchanger tubes of the main ID fan lube oil coolers, causing the massive 5MW fan bearings to overheat and trip the plant.
Action Taken: Shock-chlorination was ineffective because the biofilm protected the bacteria. We had to shut down the cooling circuit, physically scrape the cooling tower fills, and run a high-dose non-oxidizing biocide (isothiazolinone) for 48 hours to strip the biofilm from the heat exchangers.
Lesson Learned: During monsoon season (July-August), switch your biocide dosing strategy. Alternate between oxidizing (chlorine/bromine) and non-oxidizing biocides weekly so the bacteria cannot build up a resistance. Monitor the heat exchanger approach temperatures closely.
