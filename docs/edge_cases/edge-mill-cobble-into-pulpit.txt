Scenario: A high-speed bar cobble in the Wire Rod Mill directly striking the main operator pulpit.
We were rolling 12mm rebar at 20 meters per second. The entry roller guide on Stand 14 shattered. The leading edge of the red-hot bar missed the roll bite, shot vertically out of the mill stand, and speared directly toward the glass window of the elevated operator pulpit.
Action Taken: The bar struck the pulpit window violently. Thankfully, the window was made of 40mm thick bullet-resistant Lexan laminated glass, installed specifically for this edge case. The glass spider-webbed completely but held. The bar piled up into a massive glowing bird's nest on the mill floor.
Lesson Learned: Standard tempered safety glass is useless in high-speed rolling mills. Never bypass or prop open the blast shields on the pulpit windows. We subsequently redesigned the entry guide boxes on Stand 14 with a heavier cast-steel body to prevent shattering under impact.
