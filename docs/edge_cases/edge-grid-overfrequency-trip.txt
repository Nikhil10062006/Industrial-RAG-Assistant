Scenario: The state utility grid experienced a massive load rejection (a major city lost power), causing the grid frequency to spike from 50.0 Hz to 51.5 Hz.
Action Taken: Our Captive Power Plant generators were synchronized to the grid. When the frequency spiked, our turbine governors aggressively cut steam flow to try and slow the turbines down. However, the drop in steam flow meant we weren't generating enough internal power to run the steel plant. The main 11kV bus under-voltage relays tripped, blacking out the Hot Strip Mill mid-rolling.
Lesson Learned: We tightened the islanding relays. Now, if the external grid frequency deviates beyond 50.5 Hz, our main tie-breaker trips instantly, severing us from the state grid. This allows our turbines to remain stable on our internal load, saving the plant from external grid chaos.
