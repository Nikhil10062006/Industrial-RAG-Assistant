Scenario: Ladle slide gate failure. The hydraulic cylinder opened the gate, but the refractory sand in the nozzle had sintered completely solid, meaning no liquid steel would flow into the continuous caster tundish.
Action Taken: This requires emergency lancing from the bottom. This is an extremely hazardous edge case. The operator donned double aluminized PPE. You must stand offset at a 45-degree angle, never directly underneath the nozzle. Using a 3-meter oxygen lance, the operator burned upward through the solid sand plug. As soon as the red glow intensified, the lance was dropped, and the operator retreated as the steel finally broke through.
Lesson Learned: A failed open gate usually means moisture in the packing sand caused it to freeze. Always store nozzle sand in the heated ovens. Bottom lancing must only be performed by a veteran supervisor; junior staff must clear the casting floor entirely.
