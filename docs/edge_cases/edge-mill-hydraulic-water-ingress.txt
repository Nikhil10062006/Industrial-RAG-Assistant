Scenario: Massive water contamination in the Hot Strip Mill's central hydraulic reservoir.
Action Taken: To ensure smooth production line operation with routine maintenance of rolling mills including lubrication, hydraulic systems, and cooling, we run routine checks. However, we missed a microscopic crack in a water-cooled heat exchanger inside the hydraulic tank. The high-pressure cooling water forced its way into the 10,000-liter hydraulic oil reservoir, turning the oil into a thick emulsion. This destroyed the lubricity, causing five high-pressure piston pumps to fail catastrophically within 20 minutes, locking up the mill.
Lesson Learned: We permanently installed real-time water-in-oil moisture sensors on the hydraulic return lines to trigger an alarm before the pumps self-destruct.
