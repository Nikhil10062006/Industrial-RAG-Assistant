A new technician arrived at a chemical dosing skid wearing general gloves and safety glasses instead of the specified chemical gloves and face shield. The supervisor stopped the task before a drum connection was opened. The PPE matrix was posted in the control room but not at the skid, and the induction did not include a field walk. The task was re-briefed and the PPE station moved closer. Record chemical, task, missing PPE, location, and corrective action. Lesson: PPE requirements must be visible where the work occurs and matched to the chemical. General PPE is not a substitute for chemical compatibility. Field verification prevents assumptions.

The condition was added to the shift handover because it may recur under similar weather, load, material, or maintenance conditions. The immediate response protected people first, then preserved evidence for the investigation. Do not treat an unusual event as harmless because the plant recovered. Check adjacent equipment, temporary repairs, alarms, and records from the affected window. The practical lesson is to identify the early sign, the safe boundary, the escalation point, and the evidence required before normal operation is restored.
