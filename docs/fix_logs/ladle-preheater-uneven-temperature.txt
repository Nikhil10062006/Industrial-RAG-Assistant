Operating area: Steelmaking and secondary metallurgy. Do not correct several process variables at once. Make one controlled change, watch the response, and record the result. Water, oxygen, electricity, and molten steel require the specialist response even when the visible symptom looks small. A preheater corner stayed cold because one burner air register was loose. Inspect tip, air register, gas pressure, flame shape, and tundish or ladle dry-out history. The burner was not accepted until the full temperature map agreed. Record the equipment tag, material or heat identity, time, readings with units, people informed, and the final status. If a temporary control, bypass, manual setting, or alternate route is used, give it an owner and review time. Do not close the event because the alarm cleared or the equipment restarted once. Repeat the relevant test under the same load, speed, temperature, pressure, or material condition that caused the problem. At handover, state what was checked, what remains uncertain, and the exact condition that requires stopping the equipment. Practical plant knowledge is useful when it makes the next safe decision easier without replacing isolation, permit, quality, emergency, or engineering controls.
