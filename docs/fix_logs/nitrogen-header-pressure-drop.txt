Symptom: nitrogen pressure fell during a ladle purging campaign while the vaporizer and storage pressure looked normal. A relief bypass was passing and one large user valve had been left open after a test. The header was stabilized by isolating the unplanned demand and restoring the normal lineup. Leak testing found a damaged hose connection at a remote point. Record header pressure, storage pressure, vaporizer status, user demand, valve positions, and detector readings. Veteran note: a gas-supply problem can be a demand or leak problem downstream. Check the whole header and the timing of user changes before adjusting regulators. Nitrogen can displace oxygen, so leak inspection in pits or enclosed spaces requires gas testing and ventilation. Do not enter to listen for a leak without the permit.
