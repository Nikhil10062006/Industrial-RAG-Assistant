Problem: Blast Furnace tuyere 14 cooling water delta-flow alarm triggering intermittently. The HMI shows a 60 L/min difference between supply and return.
Root Cause: A 60 L/min diff normally means the copper tuyere nose is burned and leaking water into the furnace (critical hazard). However, the return temperature thermocouple showed steady at 45°C. A real leak usually causes a temperature spike before the flow drops. Inspected the local valve stand. The pneumatic bypass valve positioner was hunting due to a fouled I/P converter, causing flow surges that the ultrasonic flowmeter misread.
Fix: Bypassed the cooling circuit to the emergency raw water line temporarily. Replaced the I/P converter on the valve positioner and blew out the instrument air supply line. We get this every monsoon when moisture gets into the instrument air headers.
Pro-Tip: If the flow alarm trips but the water temp is stable, always check the valve positioner physically before calling for a massive furnace shutdown.
