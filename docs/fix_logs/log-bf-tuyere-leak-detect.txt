Problem: The Blast Furnace shift manager suspected a water leak into the furnace, as the hot metal silicon was dropping and the gas hydrogen levels were rising. However, the automated tuyere delta-flow alarms showed nothing.
Root Cause: An automated flow meter only trips if the leak exceeds 20 L/min. This was a micro-leak. We had to find it the old-fashioned way. We walked the bustle ring and performed a "bubble test" on the return lines of all 24 tuyeres. We closed the main return valve on each tuyere slightly, creating backpressure, and opened the small vent petcock into a bucket of water. Tuyere 14's petcock blew massive amounts of gas bubbles into the bucket. The copper tip was cracked, and the high-pressure furnace gas was actually blowing backward into the cooling water line.
Fix: Isolated Tuyere 14, valved it off, and pulled the blast wind down to execute an emergency tuyere change.
Pro-Tip: Never trust flowmeters for micro-leaks. The bucket bubble test on the return line vent is the only foolproof way to find a cracked copper cooler.
