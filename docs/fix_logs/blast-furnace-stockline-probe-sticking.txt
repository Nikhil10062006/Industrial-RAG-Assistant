Symptom: the stockline probe stopped changing during several charging cycles while the burden profile and hopper weights continued to move. The level signal looked plausible and did not trigger a hard fault. Field inspection was performed from the approved top-equipment access point with gas testing and protective controls. The probe sleeve had fines and moisture packed around it, and the purge line filter was nearly blocked. The probe was not forced because the drive torque was already elevated. After the sleeve was cleaned and purge restored, the probe returned smoothly. The team compared the reading with burden weight and alternate level indication before accepting it. Veteran note: a frozen stockline reading is not always a failed encoder; wet fines can hold a probe in one position. However, a stuck level signal can affect charging logic and top-pressure control, so it must be treated as a functional failure until proven otherwise. Record probe position, drive torque, purge pressure, burden movement, weather, and the point at which the signal recovered.
