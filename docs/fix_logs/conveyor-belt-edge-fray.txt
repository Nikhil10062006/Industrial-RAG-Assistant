Symptom: a conveyor belt edge began fraying near the loading point. Belt tracking was corrected temporarily, but the fray returned. Inspection found a bent impact idler bracket and a sharp skirt clamp touching the belt. The belt was isolated, the bracket replaced, and the skirt clearance reset. The splice and carcass were inspected before restart. Record belt tag, location, tracking position, load, idler condition, skirt clearance, and repair. Veteran note: repeated edge damage usually has a fixed contact point. Do not keep adjusting tracking to compensate for a bent support. Run the empty belt first, then load gradually while watching the repaired area. A frayed edge can catch on structure and create a longitudinal tear.

The equipment was not accepted merely because the alarm cleared. A controlled trial was run under the same load or process condition that produced the symptom, and the relevant trend was saved. The work record includes the tag, time, readings with units, recent maintenance, parts changed, and what remains unverified. If the condition returns, compare it with this baseline before changing another parameter. This detail matters because a short reset can hide a mechanical, process, or instrument problem.
