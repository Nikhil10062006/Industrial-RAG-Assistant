Slit widths drifted and edge burr increased. Separator discs were worn unevenly and one spacer had been installed reversed. The arbor was isolated, stack measured, and the discs replaced according to the job card. Record slit width, disc ID, wear, spacer sequence, burr, and trial result. A small stack error can affect every narrow coil. Do not adjust tension to compensate for incorrect tooling.  The equipment was not accepted only because the alarm cleared. A controlled trial was run under the same load or process condition that produced the symptom, and the trend was saved. The record states what was checked, what was changed, what remains unverified, and what would require another stop. This prevents a short reset from becoming an undocumented workaround. The handover should state the current operating mode, the last stable reading, the open risk, and the person or discipline responsible for the next check. If a temporary measure is used, give it an owner and review time. Do not leave a condition described only as “normal now”; explain what was observed, what evidence supports the decision, and what change would require stopping the equipment. This is how practical experience becomes usable plant knowledge without weakening the formal control system.
