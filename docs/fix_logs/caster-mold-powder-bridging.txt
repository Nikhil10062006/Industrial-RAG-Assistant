Symptom: mould powder level stopped falling from the hopper while the automatic addition signal remained on. Mould level and withdrawal speed were stable, but surface lubrication began to worsen. The powder had absorbed moisture and formed a bridge above the outlet. The hopper was isolated and emptied according to the powder-handling procedure. No person tapped the hopper while the vibrator was energized. The outlet screen and vibrator were inspected, and the hopper was dried before refill. Veteran note: a powder bridge can release suddenly as a slug, so do not stand below the outlet or increase vibration without checking the structure. Record powder lot, storage condition, hopper temperature, signal status, and time to recovery. Keep opened powder bags sealed and dry. If powder behavior changes after a lot change, quality and caster process owners should review it rather than treating every event as an operator error.

The equipment was not accepted merely because the alarm cleared. A controlled trial was run under the same load or process condition that produced the symptom, and the relevant trend was saved. The work record includes the tag, time, readings with units, recent maintenance, parts changed, and what remains unverified. If the condition returns, compare it with this baseline before changing another parameter. This detail matters because a short reset can hide a mechanical, process, or instrument problem.
