Problem: Coils exiting the Tandem Cold Mill (TCM) had a sticky, black carbon residue on the edges that wouldn't burn off during the batch annealing process, ruining the product for automotive customers.
Root Cause: The lab analyzed the residue and found it was burned rolling oil. The emulsion sprays on Stand 5 were functioning, but the strip wiping system was failing. The high-pressure air knives designed to blow the residual oil off the strip before it hit the tension reel were set at the wrong angle. They were blowing the oil sideways toward the edges of the strip instead of backward into the catch basin. This allowed a thick bead of oil to pool on the coil edges. When heated to 700°C in the annealing furnace, this thick oil bead cracked into solid carbon.
Fix: Realigned the air knives on Stand 5 to a strict 15-degree backward tilt. Increased the blower pressure from 3 bar to 5 bar.
Pro-Tip: The mill doesn't just need to put oil on the strip; it must take it off. If your air knives are misaligned, you are shipping dirty steel.
