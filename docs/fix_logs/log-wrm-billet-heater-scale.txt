Problem: Wire Rod Mill billets were exiting the walking hearth furnace with an abnormally thick, 5mm crust of scale, causing severe yield loss and rapid wear on the Stand 1 roughing rolls.
Root Cause: Thick scale means the steel is oxidizing heavily inside the furnace. Oxidation requires excess free oxygen. The furnace control room HMI showed an ideal 1.5% O2 level in the soak zone. However, I physically inspected the Zirconia O2 probe on the roof of the furnace. The sample extraction tube had snapped off, meaning the probe was measuring the stagnant, relatively clean air near the roof refractory, not the actual combustion gases in the chamber. The air/fuel ratio had drifted extremely lean, flooding the furnace with excess air.
Fix: Replaced the Zirconia probe and sample tube. Recalibrated the air/gas cross-limiting loop. Scale thickness dropped back to the normal 1mm.
Pro-Tip: Never blindly trust an in-situ analyzer without verifying the physical integrity of its sample probe.
