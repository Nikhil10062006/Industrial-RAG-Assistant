Symptom: basin level fell below the pump minimum during a hot afternoon, although makeup was commanded open. The makeup valve was open on the screen, but a manual upstream valve was partly closed after water-treatment work. The basin level recovered slowly after the lineup was corrected. The pump suction was inspected for air entry and vortexing before full load returned. Record basin level, makeup pressure, valve positions, pump current, and tower fan status. Do not start another pump against an empty or poorly supplied basin. Veteran note: level problems that appear only during high demand often expose a restricted makeup path, not a faulty level transmitter. Check actual flow and valve position in the field. Low basin level can damage pumps and reduce heat rejection at the same time.
