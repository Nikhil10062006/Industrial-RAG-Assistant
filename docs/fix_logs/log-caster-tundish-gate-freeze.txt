Problem: At the start of a new casting sequence, the Continuous Caster Strand 1 slide gate opened to 100%, but absolutely no steel flowed into the mold. The tundish nozzle had frozen solid.
Root Cause: The ladle operator had opened the ladle late, causing the steel level in the tundish to fill too slowly. The tundish had been preheated correctly to 1100°C, but because the fill rate was so slow, the first wash of steel lost all its superheat to the refractory. By the time it reached the nozzle well, it was below its liquidus temperature and froze solid, cementing the upper nozzle to the slide gate plate.
Fix: We had to execute a dangerous oxygen lancing from beneath the machine to burn the frozen plug out and establish flow. We lost 10 meters of slab quality and nearly aborted the strand.
Pro-Tip: Tundish fill rate is critical. Once the ladle is open, you must fill the tundish to 60% capacity within 90 seconds. If you trickle-fill it, you will freeze the nozzles every single time.
