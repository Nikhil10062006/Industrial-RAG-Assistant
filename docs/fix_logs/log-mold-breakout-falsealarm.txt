Problem: The Continuous Caster Strand 2 triggered a "Mold Breakout" alarm and slammed the slide gate shut automatically, aborting the cast.
Root Cause: The breakout detection system relies on rows of thermocouples embedded in the copper mold. It looks for a specific heat wave propagating downwards. The data showed a massive temperature spike on Thermocouple Row 1, Sensor 8, followed immediately by the alarm. However, there was no sticker or shell tear. I inspected the copper mold plate during the turnaround. A small hairline crack in the mold's water cooling jacket was spraying a microscopic jet of high-pressure cooling water directly onto the back of that specific thermocouple. The water shorted the thermocouple wiring, causing an instantaneous artificial temperature spike that the PLC interpreted as liquid steel breaching the shell.
Fix: Replaced the entire copper narrow-face plate and the damaged thermocouple wiring harness.
Pro-Tip: True breakouts show a thermal curve over 5-10 seconds. Instantaneous spikes to maximum value are always electrical shorts or water damage to the sensor.
