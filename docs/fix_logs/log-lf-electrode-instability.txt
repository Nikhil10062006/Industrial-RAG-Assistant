Problem: The Ladle Furnace (LF) Phase B electrode was "hunting" violently up and down, unable to maintain a stable electrical arc, causing severe voltage fluctuations on the plant grid.
Root Cause: The electrical regulator card was swapped, but the mast continued to bounce. The hydraulic servo valve controlling the mast was checked and found normal. The problem was mechanical friction in the mast guide rollers. The copper bus-tubes that carry 40,000 amps to the electrode act as a massive electromagnet. Over time, magnetic steel dust from the shop had gathered on the brass mast guide rails. This dust clumped up and jammed the guide rollers. The hydraulic cylinder would build up pressure to move the mast, overcome the jam with a violent jerk, overshoot the arc setpoint, and then try to correct itself, causing the bounce.
Fix: Scraped the brass guide rails clean and wiped them down with graphite dry-lube. Installed non-magnetic stainless steel shields around the roller assemblies.
Pro-Tip: On high-current equipment, magnetic dust acts like glue. Keep your guide rails spotless.
