Symptom: a pressure gauge carried a current calibration sticker, but the serial number did not match the certificate. The gauge had been moved from another service during a shutdown. The instrument was removed from release use, the correct certificate located, and process records reviewed for impact. The gauge range and connection were also checked because a similar-looking gauge had been installed previously. Record equipment tag, gauge serial, range, certificate, as-found reading, and disposition. Veteran note: a valid certificate attached to the wrong instrument is not calibration. Verify serial, range, location, and service at the point of use. Stores and maintenance were asked to control loose stickers and require serial capture in the work order.

The equipment was not accepted merely because the alarm cleared. A controlled trial was run under the same load or process condition that produced the symptom, and the relevant trend was saved. The work record includes the tag, time, readings with units, recent maintenance, parts changed, and what remains unverified. If the condition returns, compare it with this baseline before changing another parameter. This detail matters because a short reset can hide a mechanical, process, or instrument problem.
