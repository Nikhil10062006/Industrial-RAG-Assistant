An edge protector shifted during loading and exposed the strap to the coil edge. The load was stopped and repacked with the correct protector size and restraint. Record coil, protector, strap, width, load method, and inspection. Veteran packers check protector seating before the final strap tension, not after the truck leaves. Sharp edges cut straps under braking and vibration.  The equipment was not accepted only because the alarm cleared. A controlled trial was run under the same load or process condition that produced the symptom, and the trend was saved. The record states what was checked, what was changed, what remains unverified, and what would require another stop. This prevents a short reset from becoming an undocumented workaround. The handover should state the current operating mode, the last stable reading, the open risk, and the person or discipline responsible for the next check. If a temporary measure is used, give it an owner and review time. Do not leave a condition described only as “normal now”; explain what was observed, what evidence supports the decision, and what change would require stopping the equipment. This is how practical experience becomes usable plant knowledge without weakening the formal control system.
