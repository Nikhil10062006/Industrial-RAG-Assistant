Symptom: weld bead trimming became uneven and downstream sizing rolls showed periodic drag. The trim tool was sharp, but its holder had shifted after a changeover. The weld seam tracking sensor was clean and correctly centered. The line was stopped, the tool isolated, and holder runout measured. The holder was reset and the tool height matched to the weld profile. A short trial was cut and inspected before speed increased. Record tube size, weld speed, trim position, tool condition, holder runout, and defect length. Veteran note: a good cutter at the wrong height produces a poor weld trim. Do not increase tool pressure to compensate for misalignment; it can collapse the tube or damage the strip. If the defect repeats at weld pitch, check the seam tracker, holder, and weld bead rather than only the cutter insert.

The equipment was not accepted merely because the alarm cleared. A controlled trial was run under the same load or process condition that produced the symptom, and the relevant trend was saved. The work record includes the tag, time, readings with units, recent maintenance, parts changed, and what remains unverified. If the condition returns, compare it with this baseline before changing another parameter. This detail matters because a short reset can hide a mechanical, process, or instrument problem.
