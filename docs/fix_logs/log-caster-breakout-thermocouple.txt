Problem: Continuous Caster mold breakout alarm activated on Strand 2, automatically halting the strand. The alarm was triggered by a rapid temperature drop on Row 2 thermocouple #4.
Root Cause: The breakout prediction algorithm looks for a sticker (where the steel shell tears). A sticker usually shows a temperature spike, followed by a drop as the tear moves down. Here, we only saw an instant drop. Looking inside the mold, there was no shell tear. The root cause was poor mold powder application. The flux had bridged and clumped, starving that specific area of the mold of lubrication, causing the thermocouple to read the cooler flux rather than the steel shell.
Fix: Manually broke the flux bridge with a skimming rod. Added a fresh layer of reactive mold powder. Cleared the breakout alarm and cautiously restarted casting at 0.6 m/min.
Pro-Tip: An isolated temp drop without an preceding spike on adjacent thermocouples is almost always flux bridging or a dead sensor, not a true sticker breakout.
