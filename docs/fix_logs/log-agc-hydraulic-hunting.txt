Problem: Hot Strip Mill Finishing Stand F2 Automatic Gauge Control (AGC) hydraulic cylinders were hunting (oscillating) wildly by +/- 0.5mm while rolling. This created severe gauge ripples in the coil.
Root Cause: The automation team spent two hours trying to tune the PID loop gains in the software, assuming a sensor issue. I went down to the hydraulic valve stand. The Moog servo valve that controls the HAGC cylinder was incredibly hot to the touch. The root cause wasn't software; it was trapped nitrogen in the hydraulic lines. A bladder in the local accumulator had ruptured a week ago, dissolving nitrogen gas into the hydraulic oil. Under the extreme pressure of the servo valve, the gas bubbles were compressing and expanding, making the hydraulic column "spongy" and causing the cylinders to overshoot their targets.
Fix: Replaced the ruptured accumulator bladder. We then had to bleed the HAGC cylinders manually at the highest elevation vent ports for 20 minutes to get all the aerated oil out of the system.
Pro-Tip: Software doesn't suddenly go bad. If AGC is hunting out of nowhere, check your accumulator pre-charges and bleed your hydraulic lines before you touch the PLC code.
