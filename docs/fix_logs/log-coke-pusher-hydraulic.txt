Problem: The Coke Oven Pusher Machine ram was retracting extremely slowly after pushing an oven. The cycle time increased from 45 seconds to over 2 minutes, causing delays in the battery schedule.
Root Cause: Checked the main hydraulic pumps; both were running at the correct 180 bar pressure. Checked the directional proportional valve that controls ram retract. The local PLC showed a 100% command signal, but the valve spool was physically stuck at 20% open. The root cause was degraded hydraulic oil. A local filter element had collapsed weeks ago, allowing fine coal dust to bypass into the main reservoir, jamming the tight tolerances of the proportional valve.
Fix: Replaced the directional valve block. We had to drain the local HPU tank, flush it, and refill with fresh ISO 68 hydraulic oil. Replaced all inline filters.
Pro-Tip: On these pusher machines, if the filter differential pressure indicator pops out (red band), don't just push it back in. Change the filter, or you'll be changing a 5,000-dollar valve next week.
