Symptom: the bell-less chute reported a position deviation during a change in charging angle. The DCS showed the target angle, but the local position marker lagged. The motor current was normal and the operator considered resetting the drive. Inspection found looseness in the encoder coupling, allowing the feedback shaft to slip while the chute mechanism still moved. The equipment was placed in the approved safe state and the coupling was isolated before inspection. The coupling was replaced, alignment checked, and the full angle range tested at slow speed. The position feedback and mechanical pointer were then compared at several indexed positions. Tribal knowledge: if the error appears only near one angle, a mechanical coupling or cable carrier is more likely than a general drive fault. Do not force the chute through the error; a wrong charging angle can affect burden distribution and may damage the chute liner. Record angle command, actual position, motor current, alarm time, coupling condition, and verification points.
