Problem: Basic Oxygen Furnace main oxygen lance flow was hunting wildly during the main decarburization blow. Setpoint was 8000 Nm3/hr, but flow was swinging from 7500 to 8500, causing sloppy slag and heavy slopping over the vessel lip.
Root Cause: Instrument tech suspected a faulty orifice plate transmitter, but calibration checked out. The issue was upstream at the main oxygen pressure regulating valve (PRV). The pilot sensing line for the PRV was choked with fine iron oxide scale. Because the pilot line couldn't read pressure changes quickly, the main PRV valve was continually overshooting and undershooting the pressure setpoint, driving the flowmeter crazy.
Fix: Isolated the oxygen station. Removed the 1/2-inch stainless steel pilot tubing and blew it out with nitrogen. Reassembled and stroked the PRV. Flow stabilized perfectly on the next heat.
Pro-Tip: Oxygen lines generate internal rust scale over time. Any erratic flow control on the lances is usually a choked impulse or pilot line, not a failed transmitter.
