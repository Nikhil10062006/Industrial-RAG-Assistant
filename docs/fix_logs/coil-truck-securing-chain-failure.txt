Symptom: a securing chain loosened during internal transport of a coil. The coil remained on the saddle, but the chain hook had opened because the latch was bent and the chain angle was too shallow. The vehicle was stopped, the route isolated, and the coil secured with the approved spare set. The damaged chain was quarantined. Record vehicle, coil, chain ID, restraint angle, hook condition, road surface, and action. Do not retighten a damaged chain and continue. Veteran note: restraint capacity depends on angle, contact point, edge protection, and chain condition, not only chain size. Inspect hooks and latches before every load and after any shock. A coil that looks stable in the bay can move when the vehicle brakes.
