Operating area: bulk handling. Use the approved procedure, identify the equipment and energy state, preserve the first indication, and keep people outside the hazard boundary. Material began hitting the chute shell after a liner plate detached. Stop and isolate the conveyor, inspect shell, supports, belt, and remaining liners. Replace the liner and check for belt damage before loading again. Record the equipment tag, material or heat identity, time, readings with units, people informed, and the final status. If a temporary control, bypass, manual setting, or alternate route is used, give it an owner and review time. Do not close the event because the alarm cleared or the equipment restarted once. Repeat the relevant test under the same load, speed, temperature, pressure, or material condition that caused the problem. At handover, state what was checked, what remains uncertain, and the exact condition that requires stopping the equipment. Practical plant knowledge is useful when it makes the next safe decision easier without replacing isolation, permit, quality, emergency, or engineering controls.
