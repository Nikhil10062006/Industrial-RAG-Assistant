Problem: Continuous Caster Strand 1 dummy bar failed to disconnect from the hot slab at the withdrawal straightener, dragging the red-hot slab up into the dummy bar parking winch mechanism.
Root Cause: The disconnection is completely mechanical. As the slab travels through the unbending rolls, the head of the dummy bar is supposed to pivot downward, snapping the steel connection pin. In this case, the shift team used the wrong grade of steel pin during preparation. They used a high-tensile steel pin (meant for crane rigging) instead of the specified mild steel shear pin. The withdrawal straightener didn't have enough force to snap the high-tensile pin.
Fix: Absolute nightmare. We had to torch-cut the hot slab manually inside the machine while it was still attached to the dummy bar head. Replaced the warped dummy bar head and recalibrated the parking winch limit switches.
Pro-Tip: Never substitute shear pins based on what fits. A shear pin is designed to break. If you put a stronger pin in, the machine breaks instead.
