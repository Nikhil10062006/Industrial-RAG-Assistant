Symptom: a burner tripped on flame failure during stable firing. Fuel pressure, air flow, and flame appearance were normal. The scanner lens had a thin soot film and purge air was low. The burner was secured, purged, and inspected from the approved access. The lens was cleaned, purge pressure restored, and the scanner signal tested against a reference flame. Record burner number, fuel pressure, air flow, scanner signal, purge pressure, and trip time. Veteran note: a flame scanner can fail dirty while the flame is healthy, but no one should bypass flame supervision on that assumption. Check companion signals and prove the scanner before relighting. Repeated fouling may indicate burner adjustment, purge contamination, or a damaged sight tube.
