Problem: Sinter Plant Ignition Hood burners in Zone 1 kept tripping on "Flame Failure."
Root Cause: The shift electrician checked the UV flame scanners and found them covered in a thick layer of soot. He cleaned them, reset the burners, and they tripped again 10 minutes later. The scanners were working perfectly; they were correctly reading a terrible, smoky flame. The root cause was poor combustion air pressure. A bird had built a massive nest inside the intake louver of the primary combustion air fan during a three-day shutdown. The burners were starving for oxygen, producing a rich, sooty flame that blinded the scanners.
Fix: Cleared the bird's nest from the intake louver and installed a new wire mesh screen. Reset the air/gas cross-limiting controls. Burners lit off cleanly with a sharp blue flame.
Pro-Tip: If all flame scanners in a zone fail simultaneously, don't clean the scanners. Look at the flame through the peephole. If it's orange and smoky, you have an air-fuel ratio problem, usually a blocked air intake.
