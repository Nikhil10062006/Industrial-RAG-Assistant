Problem: Severe "chatter" (high-frequency vibration) on Hot Strip Mill Finishing Stand F4 while rolling 1.2mm thin gauge coils. The vibration caused visible gauge variations on the final strip and a loud howling noise in the mill bay.
Root Cause: The junior operator assumed it was wear on the backup roll bearings (a common cause). We pulled the backup rolls, but the chocks and bearings were pristine. Further investigation revealed the inter-stand strip cooling headers between F3 and F4 were partially blocked by river debris. The strip was entering F4 30°C hotter on the drive side than the operator side, causing differential thermal expansion in the roll bite and triggering a harmonic resonance.
Fix: Shut down the mill for 30 minutes. Back-flushed the inter-stand cooling headers and cleaned the y-strainers on the main water supply. Vibration vanished on the next bar.
Pro-Tip: When mill chatter only happens on thin gauges, check strip temperature uniformity before blaming the mechanical roll stack.
