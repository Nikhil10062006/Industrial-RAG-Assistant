Problem: The Continuous Caster ladle turret (which swings 300-ton ladles over the mold) began emitting a loud, rhythmic "popping" noise when rotating from the load side to the cast side.
Root Cause: The mechanical team feared the main slewing ring bearing (a 4-meter diameter bearing) was destroying itself. We halted rotation and greased the bearing heavily, but the popping continued. I checked the rotation drive pinions. The teeth were fine. We finally noticed the structural bolts holding the slewing bearing down to the concrete foundation pedestal. Ten of the two hundred high-tensile bolts had sheared off, and the remaining bolts in that quadrant had stretched. The popping noise was the massive bearing literally lifting off the pedestal by 2mm under the eccentric load of the ladle and snapping back down.
Fix: We had to shut down the caster for 48 hours. Used hydraulic tensioners to replace all 200 hold-down bolts and re-grout the base plate.
Pro-Tip: Don't ignore popping noises on heavy slewing equipment. Check the foundation bolts before assuming the bearing is dead.
