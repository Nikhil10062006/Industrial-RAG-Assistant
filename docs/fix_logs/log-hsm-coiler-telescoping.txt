Problem: Hot Strip Mill Downcoiler #1 was producing severely "telescoped" coils (the wraps were sliding sideways like a collapsed telescope instead of stacking perfectly flush).
Root Cause: The mechanical team assumed the coiler mandrel was expanding unevenly. We swapped the mandrel, but the next coil telescoped just as badly. The issue was upstream at the pinch rolls just before the coiler throat. The hydraulic servo valve controlling the drive-side pinch roll cylinder had a clogged pilot port due to dirty oil. This caused the pinch roll to clamp down harder on the operator side than the drive side, physically steering the hot strip sideways as it fed into the mandrel.
Fix: Flushed the pinch roll hydraulic valve block and replaced the Moog servo valve. We recalibrated the pinch roll gap using a load cell block to ensure perfectly symmetrical clamping force.
Pro-Tip: If a coil telescopes consistently in one direction, the mandrel is rarely the cause. You have an asymmetrical steering force upstream, usually a lopsided pinch roll or a worn side-guide plate.
