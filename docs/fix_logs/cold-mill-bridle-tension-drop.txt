Operating area: Rolling, finishing, and roll shops. Release equipment only after a trial at the same operating condition and a quality review of the affected material. Bridle tension fell when an oil film reached the roll coating. Check roll surface, tension, coolant, brake, and load-cell response before changing tension. The line was returned to automatic only after tension and shape were stable. Record the equipment tag, material or heat identity, time, readings with units, people informed, and the final status. If a temporary control, bypass, manual setting, or alternate route is used, give it an owner and review time. Do not close the event because the alarm cleared or the equipment restarted once. Repeat the relevant test under the same load, speed, temperature, pressure, or material condition that caused the problem. At handover, state what was checked, what remains uncertain, and the exact condition that requires stopping the equipment. Practical plant knowledge is useful when it makes the next safe decision easier without replacing isolation, permit, quality, emergency, or engineering controls.
