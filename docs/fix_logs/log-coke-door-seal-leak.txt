Problem: Coke Oven Battery 6 was experiencing chronic, heavy black smoke leaking from the top of the pusher-side oven doors immediately after charging coal.
Root Cause: The door cleaners were working perfectly, and battery pressure was stable. We inspected the knife-edge seals on the doors. The mechanical team had recently replaced the seals but had over-tightened the adjusting bolts on the top section. The knife-edge seal relies on a spring-loaded flexibility to conform to the warped brickwork of the oven frame. By tightening the bolts to the maximum, they made the seal completely rigid. When the door was locked into place, the rigid top seal couldn't flex into the frame's depression, leaving a 5mm gap that blew raw gas.
Fix: Backed off all the tensioning bolts on the top quadrant of the doors by two full turns to restore the spring flexibility. The seals seated perfectly on the next charge.
Pro-Tip: On coke oven doors, tighter is not better. The seal must have room to float and conform to the heat-warped frames.
