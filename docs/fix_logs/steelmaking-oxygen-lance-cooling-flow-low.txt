Symptom: lance cooling-water flow dropped during a heat while oxygen pressure remained normal. The local flow switch alarmed, but the DCS trend lagged. The suction strainer on the cooling skid was partially blocked with scale, and the standby pump isolation valve was not fully open. The lance was withdrawn according to the safe sequence and the furnace controller was informed. The water path was isolated, cleaned, and tested before the lance was returned. The oxygen line was checked separately; no oil or contamination was found. Veteran note: oxygen pressure is not evidence of lance cooling. Watch water flow, inlet pressure, outlet temperature, and leakage. A cooling failure can damage the lance and introduce water near molten steel. Record the time from alarm to withdrawal, water readings, lance condition, and the exact isolation used. If the flow signal is false, prove it with an independent indication before continuing; do not bypass a cooling interlock because the oxygen stream still looks normal.
