Symptom: a packaged coil strap broke during transfer to the storage bay. The strap specification was correct, but the strap tensioner had a worn clutch and applied uneven tension. The coil edge protector was also shifted, creating a sharp contact point. The coil was stabilized with the approved method, the area cordoned, and the package inspected before movement. The tensioner clutch and protector guides were repaired. Record coil mass, strap type, tension setting, break location, protector position, and handling action. Veteran note: a strap break is a stored-energy event. Do not approach the coil end-on or assume the broken strap is harmless after it snaps. If breaks cluster on one machine, inspect tension calibration, strap path, cutter edge, and package geometry. Quality should decide whether the coil needs repackaging or hold.
