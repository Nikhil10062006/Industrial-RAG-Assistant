Symptom: withdrawal speed feedback showed small oscillations and the strand drive produced a periodic knocking sound. Motor current was normal at low speed. Inspection during a safe maintenance window found wear in the flexible coupling spider and looseness in one hub. The encoder was healthy and had been wrongly suspected because the speed trend was noisy. The coupling was replaced, hub alignment corrected, and the drive tested at several speeds without a strand. The first heat was run at controlled speed while vibration and speed error were trended. Tribal knowledge: a repeating speed error with a mechanical knock usually belongs to the torque path before the encoder. Do not tune the speed loop around a worn coupling. Record speed, current, vibration, sound location, coupling condition, and no-load test result. The coupling guard must be restored before production; a visible rotating defect is not a reason to leave the guard open for observation.
