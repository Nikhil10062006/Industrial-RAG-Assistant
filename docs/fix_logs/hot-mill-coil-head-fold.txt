The coil head folded at the entry guide during threading. The strip was centered, but the guide gap was too narrow for the head crown after cropping. The line was stopped, the strip cleared under isolation, and the guide reset. Record coil, width, crop length, guide gap, speed, and fold location. Veteran note: a head fold is often a setup and geometry issue before it is a rolling-force issue. Thread slowly after correction and inspect the first metres.  The equipment was not accepted only because the alarm cleared. A controlled trial was run under the same load or process condition that produced the symptom, and the trend was saved. The record states what was checked, what was changed, what remains unverified, and what would require another stop. This prevents a short reset from becoming an undocumented workaround. The handover should state the current operating mode, the last stable reading, the open risk, and the person or discipline responsible for the next check. If a temporary measure is used, give it an owner and review time. Do not leave a condition described only as “normal now”; explain what was observed, what evidence supports the decision, and what change would require stopping the equipment. This is how practical experience becomes usable plant knowledge without weakening the formal control system.
